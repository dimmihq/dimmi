# Refund policy

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. The refund covers the full amount of that annual payment. To request a refund, contact support from the email address of the workspace owner.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refundable. When you cancel a monthly plan, it stays active until the end of the month you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

## Seats removed mid-period

Removing seats lowers your next invoice. Removed seats are not refunded for the current period.
