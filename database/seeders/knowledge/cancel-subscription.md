# Cancel your subscription

Only the workspace owner can cancel a subscription.

## How to cancel

1. Open **Settings**, then **Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

Your paid plan stays active until the end of the current billing period. After that the workspace moves to the Free plan; your projects and data are kept.

## Deleting the workspace

Cancelling does not delete your data. To delete the workspace and all of its data, open **Settings**, then **General**, and click **Delete workspace**.
