# Invite teammates

Workspace owners and admins can invite teammates to a workspace.

## Sending invitations

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Click **Invite members**.
3. Enter one or more email addresses and choose a role: Member or Admin.
4. Click **Send invites**.

Each invitee gets an email with a link that is valid for 7 days. On paid plans every accepted invitation adds a seat to your bill.

## Invitation did not arrive

Ask the invitee to check their spam folder. You can resend an invitation from **Settings**, then **Members**, then **Pending**.
