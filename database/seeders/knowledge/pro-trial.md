# Free trial of Pro

Every new workspace can try the Pro plan free for 14 days. No credit card is needed to start the trial.

## What happens when the trial ends

When the 14-day trial ends, your workspace moves back to the Free plan unless you add a payment method and choose Pro or Business. Nothing is deleted: projects above the Free plan limit become read-only until you upgrade.

## Extending a trial

A trial can be used once per workspace. Trials cannot be restarted from the app.
