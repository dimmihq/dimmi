# Single sign-on with SAML

Single sign-on (SSO) with SAML 2.0 is available on the Business plan only.

## Supported identity providers

Lovelace works with any SAML 2.0 identity provider, including Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace and OneLogin.

## Setting up SSO

1. Open **Settings**, then **Security**, then **Single sign-on**.
2. Copy the ACS URL and Entity ID into your identity provider.
3. Paste your provider's metadata URL into Lovelace.
4. Test the connection, then turn on **Require SSO** if every member must sign in through it.
