# Export your data and where it is stored

## CSV export

On the Pro and Business plans you can export any project to CSV. Open the project, click the **...** menu, then **Export to CSV**. The file includes tasks, assignees, statuses, due dates and comments, and opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

## Data location

All Lovelace data is hosted in the European Union, in data centers in Frankfurt, Germany. Backups are stored in the same region and kept for 30 days.
