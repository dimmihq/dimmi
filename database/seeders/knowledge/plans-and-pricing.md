# Plans and pricing

Lovelace has three plans: Free, Pro and Business. Paid plans are billed per seat, which means per teammate in your workspace, either monthly or annually.

## Free

The Free plan costs nothing and is meant for small teams trying Lovelace out. It includes up to 3 projects, up to 5 members, task boards and lists, and comments. Integrations, CSV export and SSO are not included on Free.

## Pro

The Pro plan costs $12 per seat per month. It adds unlimited projects, the Slack and GitHub integrations, CSV export, timelines and priority email support.

## Business

The Business plan costs $24 per seat per month. It includes everything in Pro plus single sign-on with SAML, audit logs, advanced permissions and a dedicated account manager.

## Price and billing questions

Prices are shown in US dollars and exclude VAT or sales tax where it applies. You can switch between plans at any time from Settings, then Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
