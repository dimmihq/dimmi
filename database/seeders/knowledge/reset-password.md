# Reset your password

## Reset steps

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you sign in with.
3. Open the reset email and click the link. The link expires after 1 hour.
4. Choose a new password with at least 10 characters.

## Reset email not received

The reset email comes from no-reply@lovelace.test. Check your spam folder. If your workspace uses SSO, passwords are managed by your identity provider and the reset link is not sent; ask your IT team instead.
