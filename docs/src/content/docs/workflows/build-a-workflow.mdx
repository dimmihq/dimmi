title: "Build a workflow"
description: "Choose a trigger, add steps and paths, and publish a workflow."
---

Press **New workflow** in **Workflows** and pick a trigger: a workflow keeps it for good, and it decides which [steps](/workflows/triggers-and-steps/) the builder offers.

## Set who the workflow runs for

Select the **Trigger** card. Its settings filter the conversations the workflow starts on:

- **Audience:** everyone, or one [saved audience](/customers/audiences/).
- **Channels:** all channels, the messenger or email.
- **When it runs:** any time, or only inside or outside [office hours](/inbox/office-hours/).
- **Once per conversation:** some triggers have this on for good.

## Add steps and paths

A step with paths sends the run down one of them.

- **Reply buttons:** 2 to 6 buttons. On a channel that cannot draw buttons, such as email, the customer answers with a button's number, its name or their own words.
- **Collect data:** saves the answer as the customer's email, name or phone. dimmi asks once more after an invalid answer. A typed email is saved unverified, and only when the customer has none.
- **Branch:** up to 10 paths. The first path whose condition matches wins, and the rest take **Everyone else**.
- **Sort by topic:** 2 to 10 categories, picked by your workspace's AI model from the customer's words.
- **Wait:** a length of time, or until office hours open.
- **Call your system:** see [Workflow requests](/developers/workflow-requests/).

## Use variables in messages

Messages, notes and request bodies take variables:

```text
Hi {{contact.first_name | fallback: "there"}}, your order {{run.vars.order_number}} is on its way.
```

`contact.custom.<key>` reads a customer [attribute](/customers/attributes-and-events/). `run.vars.<key>` reads a value an earlier step saved. A variable with no value and no fallback leaves nothing.

## Publish

**Publish** is disabled while the count beside it, such as **2 to fix**, is not zero. Press the count to jump to the first problem.

After a workflow is live, edits stay in a draft until you press **Publish changes**. [How workflows work](/workflows/how-workflows-work/) covers versions, priority between workflows and the run history.
