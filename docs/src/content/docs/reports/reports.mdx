---
title: "What each report measures"
description: "Every report in dimmi, what each metric means and how it is calculated."
---

**Reports** in the sidebar holds nine reports. Every number comes from the same per-conversation facts, which dimmi works out again within seconds of each change.

## Rules for every report

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Access | Reports show to members with `conversations:read`. A teammate who can see only some conversations gets numbers for only those. |
| Range | Whole days in the workspace's time zone. The default is the last 30 days and the longest range is 400 days. |
| **Compare** | Adds the period of the same length that ended where the range starts. |
| **Office hours** | Counts durations only while the inbox's [office hours](/inbox/office-hours/) were open. |
| Filters | **Inbox**, **Team**, **Teammate**, **Channel**, **Tag**, **Priority** and **AI**. |
| Durations | The headline is the median, with the 90th percentile and the mean beside it. |
| Dates | A number counts on the day it ends: a new conversation on its start, a close on the last close, a first response on the reply, a rating on the day it was given. |
| Left out | Spam, test, deleted and merged-away conversations. |
| Drill-down | Select a number to list the conversations behind it, up to 10,000. |

## Shared definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Start | The first customer message, or the creation time when the customer never wrote. |
| First response | From the first customer message to the first public reply by a teammate. dimmi agent answers, automatic messages and notes do not count. |
| First answer | As first response, but a dimmi agent answer counts. |
| Reply time | From the first customer message nobody answered to the next teammate reply, for each customer turn. |
| Resolution time | From the start to the last close. A reopened conversation counts again only when it closes again. |
| First contact resolution | Closed conversations that never reopened and had at most one teammate reply, as a share of closed conversations. |
| CSAT | [Ratings](/reports/conversation-ratings/) of 4 or 5, as a share of all ratings. |

## Overview

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **New conversations** | Conversations started in the range. |
| **Closed** | Conversations whose last close is in the range. |
| **First response**, **Resolution time**, **CSAT**, **First contact resolution** | As defined above. |
| **Busiest hours** | New conversations by weekday and hour. |
| **Backlog at the end of each day** | Conversations open, unassigned and waiting over a week, counted when each day ends in the workspace's time zone. |

## Responsiveness

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **First response**, **First answer, AI included**, **Reply time** | As defined above, each day and by channel, inbox, team and priority. |
| **First responses within an hour** | First responses of 3,600 seconds or less, as a share of all first responses. |

## Resolution

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **Time to first close** | From the start to the first close. |
| **Reopen rate** | Closed conversations that had reopened, as a share of closed conversations. |
| **Closed**, **Resolution time**, **First contact resolution** | As defined above. |

## Team and teammates

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **Teammates who replied** | Teammates with at least one public reply in the range. |
| **Conversations replied to**, **Replies** | Conversations a teammate replied in, and their replies. |
| **Closed by a teammate** | Closes credited to the teammate who held the conversation when it closed. |
| **Time held open** | How long a teammate held open conversations assigned to them. |
| **CSAT**, **Ratings** | Ratings credited to the teammate the customer rated. |
| **Open now** | Conversations assigned to the teammate that are not closed, whatever the range. |

## Live workload

This report ignores the range and refreshes every 30 seconds.

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **Open**, **Snoozed** | Conversations in each status now. |
| **Unassigned** | Open conversations with no teammate assigned. |
| **Waiting for a first response** | Open conversations without a first response. |
| **Oldest open**, **Median open age** | Time since open conversations started. |

## Satisfaction

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **CSAT** | As defined above. |
| **Average rating** | The mean score, from 1 to 5. |
| **Ratings of 1 or 2** | A count. |
| CSAT by teammate | Each rating goes to the teammate it rated, or to dimmi when no teammate replied. |

## dimmi agent

This report follows the conversations started in the range.

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **AI involvement** | Conversations the dimmi agent answered in, as a share of conversations started. |
| **AI resolution rate** | Resolutions as a share of conversations the agent answered in. A resolution has no teammate reply and no handover, and the customer confirmed it, the conversation closed, or the customer stayed quiet for 24 hours after the last answer. |
| **Confirmed resolutions** | The customer said an answer helped, or rated 4 or 5. |
| **Assumed resolutions** | Resolutions that are not confirmed. |
| **Handover rate** | Conversations the agent [handed over](/ai/handover/), as a share of those it answered in. |
| **Cost per resolution** | The AI cost of the conversations started in the range, divided by resolutions. |
| **AI spend** | All AI cost in the range. |
| **Questions the AI could not answer** | The 15 most asked. |

## Help center

This report needs `articles:read`. The conversation filters do not apply to it.

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **Article views** | Times an article was opened, on the web and in the messenger. dimmi keeps daily counts only, with no reader or session. |
| **Searches**, **Searches with no result** | Reader searches, and the share that found nothing. |
| **Most read articles** | Views, with the share of readers who said the article helped. |

## Tickets and SLAs

This report needs `tickets:read`.

| Metric | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets created**, **Tickets resolved** | Counted in the range. **Open tickets** counts now. |
| **Service levels kept** | [SLA](/tickets/sla-policies/) targets met, as a share of targets met or breached. A target counts on the day its clock ended. |
| **Time in state** | For tickets created in the range, the time in each state, with repeat visits added up. Time spent resolved is left out. |
| **Tracker impact** | [Trackers](/tickets/trackers/) ranked by the monthly revenue of the companies they reach, then by conversations. |

## Export and email

- **CSV** on a chart downloads that chart's data.
- **Export facts CSV** builds one row per conversation started in the range, with the same filters. Download it from **Settings → Exports**. Times are UTC and durations are seconds.
- **Email digest** mails you the **Overview**: **Daily** for the day before, or **Weekly** on Monday for the week before, at 8:00 in your time zone.
