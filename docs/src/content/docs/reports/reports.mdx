title: "What each report measures"
description: "See every report in dimmi, how each metric is calculated, and how to drill down, export CSVs, or schedule email digests."
---

Open **Reports** in the sidebar to inspect your inbox, team, AI agent, help center, and tickets. Every chart pulls from the same conversation facts, which update within seconds of each change.

## Rules shared by every report

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | Visible to members with `conversations:read`. Teammates with limited inbox access only see stats for their conversations. |
| **Date range** | Whole days in your workspace's time zone. Defaults to the last 30 days, up to 400 days. |
| **Compare** | Overlays the previous period of the same length. |
| **Office hours** | Counts durations only during each inbox's [office hours](/inbox/office-hours/). |
| **Filters** | Filter by **Inbox**, **Team**, **Teammate**, **Channel**, **Tag**, **Priority**, and **AI**. |
| **Durations** | Shows the median as the headline, with the 90th percentile and mean beside it. |
| **Dates** | Events count on the day they happen: new threads on start, closes on last close, first responses on that reply, ratings on the day submitted. |
| **Excluded** | Spam, test, deleted, and merged-away conversations are left out. |
| **Drill-down** | Click any number to open the matching conversations (up to 10,000). |

## Core definitions

| Term | How dimmi calculates it |
| --- | --- |
| **Start** | The customer's first message, or thread creation time if the customer never wrote. |
| **First response** | Time from the customer's first message to the first public teammate reply. AI replies, automated messages, and notes do not count. |
| **First answer** | Same as first response, but dimmi agent replies count too. |
| **Reply time** | Time from an unanswered customer message to the next teammate reply, for each turn. |
| **Resolution time** | Time from start to the last close. Reopened threads count again when they close again. |
| **First contact resolution** | Share of closed threads that never reopened and took at most 1 teammate reply. |
| **CSAT** | Share of [ratings](/reports/conversation-ratings/) that are 4 or 5. |

## Overview

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **New conversations** | Conversations started in the date range. |
| **Closed** | Conversations whose last close fell in the range. |
| **First response**, **Resolution time**, **CSAT**, **First contact resolution** | Uses the core definitions above. |
| **Busiest hours** | New conversations grouped by weekday and hour. |
| **Backlog at the end of each day** | Threads open, unassigned, and waiting over 1 week at day's end in your workspace's time zone. |

## Responsiveness

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **First response**, **First answer, AI included**, **Reply time** | Tracked daily and broken down by channel, inbox, team, and priority. |
| **First responses within an hour** | Share of first responses answered in 3,600 seconds (1 hour) or less. |

## Resolution

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Time to first close** | Time from start to the first time the thread closed. |
| **Reopen rate** | Share of closed conversations that had reopened at least once. |
| **Closed**, **Resolution time**, **First contact resolution** | Uses the core definitions above. |

## Team and teammates

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Teammates who replied** | Teammates who sent at least 1 public reply in the range. |
| **Conversations replied to**, **Replies** | Threads each teammate replied in, and total replies sent. |
| **Closed by a teammate** | Closes credited to whoever held the thread when it closed. |
| **Time held open** | Total time a teammate held open threads assigned to them. |
| **CSAT**, **Ratings** | Ratings credited to the teammate the customer rated. |
| **Open now** | Non-closed conversations currently assigned to the teammate (ignores date range). |

## Live workload

Live workload ignores the date range and refreshes every 30 seconds.

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Open**, **Snoozed** | Conversations in each status right now. |
| **Unassigned** | Open conversations with no teammate assigned. |
| **Waiting for a first response** | Open conversations that have not received a first response yet. |
| **Oldest open**, **Median open age** | How long open conversations have been open. |

## Satisfaction

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **CSAT** | Share of ratings scored 4 or 5. |
| **Average rating** | Mean score from 1 to 5. |
| **Ratings of 1 or 2** | Total count of negative ratings. |
| **CSAT by teammate** | Credited to the rated teammate, or to dimmi when no teammate replied. |

## dimmi agent

This report tracks conversations started in the selected date range.

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **AI involvement** | Share of started conversations where the dimmi agent replied. |
| **AI resolution rate** | Share of AI-handled threads with no teammate reply and no handover, where the customer confirmed help, the thread closed, or the customer stayed quiet for 24 hours after the last reply. |
| **Confirmed resolutions** | Resolutions where the customer confirmed the answer helped or rated 4 or 5. |
| **Assumed resolutions** | Resolutions that were not explicitly confirmed. |
| **Handover rate** | Share of AI-handled threads the agent [handed over](/ai/handover/). |
| **Cost per resolution** | Total AI cost of started threads divided by AI resolutions. |
| **AI spend** | Total AI cost across the date range. |
| **Questions the AI could not answer** | The 15 most frequent unanswered questions. |

## Help center

Requires `articles:read`. Conversation filters do not apply here.

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Article views** | Total opens on the web and in the messenger. Stored as daily totals only, with no reader or session tracking. |
| **Searches**, **Searches with no result** | Total searches and the share that returned zero articles. |
| **Most read articles** | Views alongside the share of readers who marked each article helpful. |

## Tickets and SLAs

Requires `tickets:read`.

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| **Tickets created**, **Tickets resolved** | Counted in the selected date range. **Open tickets** shows current open count. |
| **Service levels kept** | Share of [SLA](/tickets/sla-policies/) targets met vs. breached, counted on the day each clock ended. |
| **Time in state** | Total time tickets created in the range spent in each state (excluding resolved time). |
| **Tracker impact** | [Trackers](/tickets/trackers/) ranked by affected company monthly revenue, then by conversation count. |

## Exports and email digests

- **Chart CSV:** click **CSV** on any chart to download its rows.
- **Export facts CSV:** exports one row per conversation started in your range with your active filters. Download it from **Settings → Exports** (timestamps in UTC, durations in seconds).
- **Email digest:** sends the **Overview** report to your inbox at 8:00 in your time zone, either **Daily** (for yesterday) or **Weekly** on Monday (for last week).
