---
title: "Ask for conversation ratings"
description: "Turn on CSAT ratings when conversations close, pick the delay and channels, and use ratings in workflows."
---

Turn on **Ask for a rating when a conversation closes** in **Settings → Conversation ratings**. It starts turned off in a new workspace.

Customers pick one of five faces (scored 1 to 5) and can leave an optional comment.

## When dimmi asks for a rating

- **Channels:** works on messenger and email threads. Both turn on once you enable ratings.
- **In the messenger:** shows a 5-face rating card at the end of the thread.
- **Over email:** sends a reply in the same email thread from your conversation's sending address. Each face links to a rating page with no sign-in required.
- **Delay:** choose **Straight away**, **After 10 minutes**, or **After an hour**. If the thread reopens before the delay finishes, dimmi cancels the ask.
- **dimmi agent closes:** threads closed by the agent only ask when **Include conversations closed by the agent** is turned on.
- **Test threads:** never receive rating requests.

dimmi skips sending an email ask if your workspace has no sending address, email sending is paused, or the customer's address is missing or suppressed.

## After a customer rates

- **Editing window:** customers can update their score and comment for 7 days after the ask goes out.
- **Conversation timeline:** each rating appears in the timeline under the customer's name.
- **Report credit:** credited to the teammate assigned when the thread closed, or to dimmi if no teammate replied. See [Satisfaction](/reports/reports/#satisfaction).
- **Webhooks:** fires [`conversation.rated`](/developers/webhooks/events/) each time a customer submits or updates a rating.

## Use ratings in workflows

- **Trigger:** **Customer rates the conversation** starts a [workflow](/workflows/triggers-and-steps/) on the first rating only, so edits do not trigger it twice.
- **Close step:** turning on **Ask for a rating** inside a workflow's **Close** step sends an ask even if global rating settings would skip it (as long as the thread is on messenger or email).
