---
title: "Add knowledge sources"
description: "Connect your website, docs, Notion, GitHub, Intercom, or Zendesk so the dimmi agent and Operator can answer from them."
---

The dimmi agent already reads your help center. When your answers live in other tools too, admins can connect them in **Settings → Knowledge**.

For GitHub, Notion, Intercom, or Zendesk, connect the account in **Settings → Integrations** first. One workspace connects one account of each tool, and your knowledge sources read through it.

## Source types

Pick the source that matches where your docs live.

| Source | What dimmi reads | Sync options (default first) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Website** | The pages you tick from the site's sitemap or the links on its start URL. The top 20 pages start ticked, and one source reads 100 at most. | **Every week**, **Every day**, **Only when I sync** |
| **Markdown files** | A zip of Markdown files, or one file. Up to 50 MB and 2,000 files. | Upload again to replace the files. |
| **GitHub** | Markdown files on one branch of a repository. | **Every day**, **Every hour**, **Every week**, **Only when I sync** |
| **Notion** | The pages you share with dimmi and tick. | **Every day**, **Every week**, **Only when I sync** |
| **Intercom** | Help center articles, with translations. | **Only when I sync**, **Every day**, **Every week** |
| **Zendesk** | Help center articles, with translations. | **Only when I sync**, **Every day**, **Every week** |

Each source holds up to 5,000 documents, and a website source up to 100 pages. The website crawler follows `robots.txt` and fetches at most one page per second from each host.

A website is never read whole. dimmi lists its pages without reading them, ticks the 20 nearest the home page, and reads only what stays ticked. To read more, open the source and tick pages or folders under **Content**.

## Synced copy vs. help center articles

When you add a source, choose how dimmi stores it:

- **Synced copy:** dimmi keeps a copy up to date automatically. Turn on **Agent** so the dimmi agent answers from it, and **Operator** so [Operator](/operator/work-with-operator/) can read it too.
- **Help center articles:** dimmi imports the files once as articles you can edit. Folders become collections, images copy over, and old URLs redirect to the new articles. Websites cannot be imported this way. Translated articles become [translations](/help-center/languages/) of the article they translate, in the same help center, and dimmi turns their language on.

You can also click **Turn into articles** on a synced source later. Importing again updates the same articles instead of making copies.

## How syncs work

Each source runs one sync at a time. A sync only reads pages that changed and removes pages that were deleted at the source.

- **Stalled syncs:** dimmi marks a sync as failed if it makes no progress for 30 minutes.
- **Sync history:** keeps the last 50 runs so you can spot what changed or failed.

## Leave documents out

Open the **Excluded** filter on any source to see pages dimmi skipped and why. Common reasons are `robots.txt`, pages that need JavaScript to render, empty pages, duplicates, or drafts.

You can also exclude any page yourself from the list. It stays excluded through future syncs until you include it again.
