Once a [procedure](/ai/procedures/) is live, dimmi tracks every published version and every conversation that follows it. You can compare changes step by step, undo a bad edit in seconds, and inspect where live chats succeed or hand over.

## Version history and rollback

Every time you click **Publish** or **Publish changes**, dimmi saves a numbered version with who published it and the note they left.

1. **Compare pickers:** pick any two versions, or compare an older version against your **Working copy**.
2. **Step-by-step diff:** highlights added, removed, and changed steps in plain words, including rule edits.
3. **Version menu (`⋯`):** compare a version with your working copy, **Restore as working copy** to inspect it before publishing, or **Publish this version** to make it live right away after confirming.

## Procedure analytics

Open the **Analytics** tab to inspect how a procedure performed over the last **7**, **30**, or **90** days, across all versions or one specific version:

- **Conversations and Resolved by AI:** counted the same way as **Reports → dimmi agent**, so workspace totals always match.
- **Handed over and CSAT:** shows handover rate alongside positive rating share and average score out of 5.
- **Steps:** tracks how many conversations reached and finished each step, plus how often each branch went **Then** versus **Else**.
- **Handovers by reason:** separates the procedure's own handover rules from safety-check handovers and errors.
- **Guardrails:** counts out-of-order calls refused, replies sent back for a skipped action, and safety handovers.
- **Actions and recent runs:** shows each action's speed and error rate, plus clickable links to the exact conversations behind the numbers.

To compare every procedure side by side, open **Reports → dimmi agent** and scroll to the **Procedures** table. Click any row to jump straight to that procedure's **Analytics** tab.

## Audit procedures with Operator

Click **Operator** in the procedure editor header to work on a live playbook with [Operator](/operator/work-with-operator/). Three built-in prompts are ready right below the composer:

- **Review recent runs:** reviews recent runs that failed or triggered safety handovers, then proposes working-copy edits plus a saved simulation made from the failing chat in one reviewable [changeset](/operator/changesets/).
- **Review this procedure for gaps:** flags unclear steps, missing edge cases, and policy conflicts.
- **Suggest test cases:** proposes saved [simulations](/ai/test-procedures/) covering your main branches and edge cases.

To run failure audits automatically, turn on **Review procedures every week** under **Settings → Operator**. Every Monday, Operator reviews recent failing procedure runs and prepares reviewable changesets for your team. Operator never publishes a procedure on its own.

For programmatic access to versions, metrics, and the workspace procedures report, check the `ai:read` and `ai:write` endpoints in the [API reference](/api-reference/) or connect over [MCP](/developers/mcp/).
