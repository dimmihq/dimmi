dimmi attempts every webhook delivery up to 12 times over 24 hours.

## How dimmi treats your response

| Your server's response | What dimmi does |
| --- | --- |
| `2xx` status | Marks the delivery successful. |
| `3xx` redirect | Marks failed (dimmi does not follow redirects). |
| `410 Gone` | Marks failed and disables the endpoint right away. |
| Any other error, connection failure, or no response within 15 seconds | Marks failed and schedules a retry. |

## Retry schedule

| Attempt | Time after first attempt |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Right away |
| 2 | 30 seconds |
| 3 | 2 minutes |
| 4 | 10 minutes |
| 5 | 30 minutes |
| 6 | 1 hour |
| 7 | 2 hours |
| 8 | 4 hours |
| 9 | 8 hours |
| 10 | 14 hours |
| 11 | 20 hours |
| 12 | 24 hours |

If your server returns a `Retry-After` header (in seconds or HTTP date format), dimmi waits at least that long before retrying.

After 12 failed attempts, retries stop. You can replay any failed delivery manually with **Send again** or via [Create webhook delivery redelivery](/api-reference/webhooks/create-webhook-delivery-redelivery/).

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Max delivery rate per endpoint | 25 requests / second |
| Max payload size | 256 KiB |
| Delivery log retention | 30 days |

Payloads larger than 256 KiB arrive with `truncated: true`, and `data.object` contains only `id`, `object`, and `links.self` (when available).

## Auto-disabling broken endpoints

| Failure duration | Action taken |
| --- | --- |
| 24 hours | Emails workspace Owners and Admins. |
| 72 hours | Disables the endpoint, cancels queued retries, and emails Owners and Admins. |

When you re-enable an endpoint, dimmi delivers new events going forward.
