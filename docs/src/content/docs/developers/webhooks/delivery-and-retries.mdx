dimmi tries each webhook delivery up to 12 times over 24 hours.

## What counts as delivered

| Your server's answer | Result |
| --- | --- |
| A `2xx` status | Delivered. |
| A `3xx` redirect | Failed. dimmi does not follow redirects. |
| `410 Gone` | Failed, and dimmi turns the endpoint off at once. |
| Another status, a connection error, or no answer within 15 seconds | Failed, then retried. |

## Retry schedule

| Attempt | After the first attempt |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately |
| 2 | 30 seconds |
| 3 | 2 minutes |
| 4 | 10 minutes |
| 5 | 30 minutes |
| 6 | 1 hour |
| 7 | 2 hours |
| 8 | 4 hours |
| 9 | 8 hours |
| 10 | 14 hours |
| 11 | 20 hours |
| 12 | 24 hours |

A `Retry-After` header in your answer, in seconds or as an HTTP date, delays the next attempt by at least that long.

After attempt 12 fails, dimmi stops. To send the delivery again, click **Send again** or call [Create webhook delivery redelivery](/api-reference/webhooks/create-webhook-delivery-redelivery/).

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rate per endpoint | 25 requests per second |
| Largest body | 256 KiB |
| Delivery log | Kept 30 days |

A larger event has `truncated: true`, and its `data.object` holds only `id`, `object` and, where available, `links.self`.

## When dimmi turns an endpoint off

| Endpoint failing for | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 24 hours | Owners and Admins get an email. |
| 72 hours | dimmi turns the endpoint off, cancels its waiting deliveries and emails Owners and Admins. |

After you turn an endpoint back on, dimmi sends new events only.
