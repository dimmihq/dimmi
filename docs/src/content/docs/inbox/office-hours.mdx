---
title: "Set office hours and availability"
description: "Set your team's schedule and individual teammate availability so SLAs, workflows, assignment, and the dimmi agent stay accurate."
---

**Office hours** tell dimmi when your team works, while **availability** tells dimmi whether a specific teammate can take new chats right now. Manage workspace schedules in **Settings → Office hours**, and change your own status under **Availability** in your account menu.

## Office hours

New workspaces start with a default schedule of **Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00** in your workspace time zone. Adding a holiday marks that entire date closed.

Three parts of dimmi follow your office hours:

- **dimmi agent:** if set to reply only during or outside business hours, it reads the hours of the conversation's inbox.
- **Workflows:** conditions and wait timers can count business hours only. Schedule edits apply to new waits started after you save.
- **SLA policies:** each [SLA policy](/tickets/sla-policies/) counts reply and resolution clocks using the schedule you pick for it.

The settings page edits your default schedule. To add extra regional schedules or give an inbox its own hours, use the REST API. You cannot delete the default schedule or any schedule still attached to an inbox or workflow.

## Teammate availability

Each teammate chooses one of three statuses:

| Status | Effect on your queue |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | [Automatic assignment](/inbox/how-assignment-works/) can route new conversations to you. |
| **Away** | Automatic assignment skips you (unless the inbox turns on **Include away teammates**). You keep your current chats. |
| **Away and reassigning** | Same as **Away**, plus any open chat you hold leaves your queue if the customer writes back so your team can pick it up. |

A few details to keep in mind:

- **Reassigned chats stay moved:** conversations that left you during **Away and reassigning** do not bounce back when you switch to **Active**.
- **Messenger presence:** customers only see you as online in the messenger when you are **Active** and have dimmi open.
- **Changing someone else's status:** requires the **Manage availability** permission in your [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/).
