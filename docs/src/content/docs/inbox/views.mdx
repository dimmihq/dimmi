---
title: "Create views"
description: "Save a filtered list of conversations as a view, and share it with your team."
---

To create a view, open **Filter** above a conversation list, add conditions, name the filter and choose **Save as view**. **Apply** filters the list once without saving.

## Conditions

- Conditions sit in groups. A group matches when all or any of its conditions do, and the groups join the same way.
- A view holds up to 5 groups.
- A new view keeps the status and sort of the list you started from.
- When a condition names a team or inbox that was deleted, the condition matches nothing and the editor flags it.

## Who sees a view

- **Only me:** a personal view. Each teammate keeps up to 50.
- **Everyone:** a shared view. A workspace keeps up to 200, and creating or changing one needs the **Manage shared views** permission from your [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/).

You choose who sees a view when you create it, and the editor does not change it afterward. To share a personal view, create it again as **Everyone**.

A name is up to 60 characters. Two shared views cannot share a name, and neither can two of your own.

## Pin, duplicate and filter by agent

- **Pin view** keeps a view in your sidebar. Pins are yours alone.
- **Duplicate view** makes a personal copy named "Copy of" and the view's name.
- **Filter by agent** narrows any list to **Agent involved**, **Agent answering** or **Handed over by agent**. dimmi does not save this filter with a view.
