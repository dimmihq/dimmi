---
title: "Create views"
description: "Save filtered conversation lists as personal or shared views and pin your favorites to the sidebar."
---

As your queue grows, default lists like **Unassigned** are not enough. **Views** let you save any filtered list of conversations, such as "Urgent billing chats" or "Open Enterprise bugs", so your team can jump straight to what matters.

To make one, click **Filter** above any conversation list, add your conditions, give it a name, and click **Save as view**. Use **Apply** instead if you want to filter the list once without saving it.

## Building filter conditions

Combine conditions to narrow a list down to the exact threads you want:

- **Grouped logic:** group conditions together with **all** (AND) or **any** (OR), and join up to **5 groups** the same way.
- **Keeps your starting state:** a new view saves the status tab and sort order of the list you started from.
- **AI attributes:** filter by any of your [AI attributes](/ai/ai-attributes/), such as every conversation whose intent is `refund`.
- **Deleted references flagged:** if a condition points to a team or inbox that was later deleted, dimmi flags it in the editor so you can fix it.

## Personal vs. shared views

Pick who can see a view when you create it:

- **Only me:** a personal view only you see (up to **50** per teammate).
- **Everyone:** a shared view for the whole workspace (up to **200** per workspace). Creating or editing shared views requires the **Manage shared views** permission in your [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/).

Visibility is set at creation time and cannot be switched later. If you build a personal view your whole team wants, recreate it as **Everyone**.

## Pinning, duplicating, and filtering by AI

- **Pin view:** keep your most-used views at the top of your sidebar. Pins are personal and never change anyone else's sidebar.
- **Duplicate view:** clone an existing view into a personal copy named `"Copy of <name>"` so you can tweak filters safely.
- **Filter by agent:** narrow any list to **Agent involved**, **Agent answering**, or **Handed over by agent**. This quick filter is temporary and is not saved inside a view.
