---
title: "How automatic assignment works"
description: "How dimmi picks a teammate for a conversation: eligibility, capacity, availability and what happens when nobody is free."
---

In an inbox set to **Round robin** or **Balanced**, dimmi gives each conversation nobody holds to one eligible teammate, or makes it wait until one frees up. Set the mode in [the inbox's settings](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/).

## When dimmi assigns

dimmi looks for a teammate when a conversation without an assignee:

- is started by a customer
- reopens
- moves to another team
- leaves a teammate who is **Away and reassigning**

dimmi skips a conversation that is closed, snoozed, spam or deleted, that the dimmi agent or a workflow still handles, or whose customer is blocked.

## Who is eligible

The pool is the conversation's team. Without one it is the inbox's **Default team**, and without that every active teammate.

A teammate in the pool is eligible when they are:

- **Active**, or away while the inbox has **Include away teammates** on
- under their capacity: their own limit when they have one, the inbox's **Capacity** otherwise
- under the limit for the conversation's channel, when one is set

Capacity counts open conversations. Snoozed, closed, spam and deleted conversations do not count.

## Who dimmi picks

- **Round robin:** the next eligible teammate after the last one picked, in a fixed order.
- **Balanced:** the eligible teammate with the fewest open conversations. On a tie, the one whose last automatic assignment is oldest.

## When nobody is eligible

1. dimmi tries the inbox's **Overflow team** once, by the same rules.
2. If nobody there can take it, the conversation keeps its team and waits. Its timeline says why.
3. dimmi tries again when a teammate closes, snoozes, deletes or hands off a conversation, or changes availability, and every minute.

Waiting conversations go out in the inbox's **Queue order**. A teammate can still take or assign a waiting conversation by hand.
