---
title: "How the inbox works"
description: "How customer conversations arrive in dimmi, move between open, snoozed, and closed, and get assigned to teammates."
---

Every channel connection in dimmi (a messenger, support email, Slack channel, or API source) delivers new chats into one inbox. Inside the inbox, every conversation has one status: **open**, **snoozed**, or **closed**.

Spam and trash sit beside those statuses as separate flags so you can clean up junk or recover a deleted thread without losing history.

## Sidebar views

Your sidebar groups conversations into built-in lists, followed by your [saved views](/inbox/views/) and quick views for each ticket type, team, and teammate.

- **Unassigned:** open chats nobody holds yet. Hidden from teammates whose role restricts access to unassigned threads.
- **Snoozed:** only the snoozed chats assigned to you, keeping other people's reminders out of your way.
- **Deleted:** trashed conversations, shown only to roles allowed to delete threads.
- **Inboxes:** appears once your workspace has **2 or more inboxes**, giving each inbox its own list.

## Closing and reopening

When a customer replies to a closed thread within the inbox's **Reopen window** (**30 days** by default), dimmi reopens that same conversation. If they write back after the window passes, dimmi opens a fresh conversation instead.

Automated emails like out-of-office auto-replies never reopen a closed thread or wake a snoozed one.

## Snoozing a conversation

Snoozing hides a waiting thread until it is time to follow up. Presets use your personal time zone (or the workspace time zone if yours is unset):

| Preset | Wakes up |
| --- | --- |
| **Later today** | In **3 hours**, rounded up to the next quarter hour |
| **Tomorrow** | Tomorrow at **09:00** |
| **Next week** | Next Monday at **09:00** |
| **In one week** | In **7 days** at **09:00** |

A few helpful rules to know:

- **Customer replies wake it early:** if the customer writes back before the timer ends, the chat moves right back to **open**. By default, a teammate's reply wakes it too.
- **Custom snooze range:** pick any custom time from **1 minute** up to **1 year** out.
- **Open threads only:** reopen a closed conversation before snoozing it.

## Who owns a conversation

When a teammate replies to an unassigned conversation, dimmi assigns it to them so two people never answer the same customer twice. You can change that toggle (**Assign to whoever replies**) or turn on automatic routing in [Inboxes and assignment](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/).

## Spam and trash

- **Spam:** marking a thread as spam moves future conversations from that customer straight into **Spam**. Blocked customers land there too.
- **Deleted:** deleting a conversation removes it from normal queues and counts, but keeps its messages so an authorized teammate can restore it later.
