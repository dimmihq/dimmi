---
title: "Use saved replies"
description: "Build reusable replies with customer variables and automatic follow-up actions, then share them with your team."
---

When your team answers the same refund, password reset, or setup question ten times a day, **saved replies** turn that response into a two-second shortcut. Manage them in **Settings → Saved replies**.

Inside any conversation composer, open the add menu or type `#` followed by the reply's shortcut to drop it in.

## Personalizing with variables

Click **Variable** in the saved reply editor to insert customer or teammate fields. dimmi fills every variable the moment you insert the reply into the composer, so you can read and tweak the final text before sending.

| Variable | Inserts |
| --- | --- |
| `{{ contact.first_name }}` | Customer's first name |
| `{{ contact.name }}` | Customer's full name |
| `{{ contact.email }}` | Customer's email address |
| `{{ company.name }}` | Customer's company name |
| `{{ sender.first_name }}` | Your first name |

Add a fallback whenever a field might be empty:

```text
Hi {{ contact.first_name | default: "there" }}, here is your invoice receipt.
```

## Pairing replies with actions

Under **When it is sent**, a saved reply can also tidy up the thread automatically: change status, set priority, assign a teammate or team, or add up to **10 tags**.

- **Runs with your permissions:** dimmi applies actions right after your reply sends. If your role lacks permission for one action, your reply still sends while that action is skipped.
- **Send buttons win:** if you pick **Send and close** or **Send and snooze** in the composer, your choice overrides the saved reply's status action.

## Sharing with your team

- **Only me:** appears only in your own composer.
- **Everyone:** shared across the whole workspace. Requires the **Manage shared replies** permission in your [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/).
