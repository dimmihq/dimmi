---
title: "Set up inboxes and assignment"
description: "Create inboxes and choose how dimmi assigns their conversations: manually, round robin or balanced."
---

Create and change inboxes in **Settings → Inboxes**. Each channel connection, such as one messenger or one support address, sends its new conversations to one inbox.

## Inbox settings

- **Default team:** new conversations go to this team, and its members form the pool for automatic assignment. With **No team**, the pool is every active teammate.
- **Tickets:** every new conversation becomes a [customer ticket](/tickets/how-tickets-work/) of the type you pick.
- **Access:** with **Chosen teams and teammates**, only the people on the list, owners and admins can open the inbox and its conversations.
- **Assign to whoever replies:** on in a new inbox.
- **Reopen window:** how long after closing a customer's reply reopens the conversation. The default is 30 days.

The default inbox cannot be deleted. Deleting another inbox moves its conversations and channels to the inbox you pick.

## Automatic assignment

| Mode | What dimmi does |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual** | Assigns nobody. The default. |
| **Round robin** | Gives each new conversation to the next teammate in the pool. |
| **Balanced** | Gives each new conversation to the teammate with the fewest open conversations. |

[How automatic assignment works](/inbox/how-assignment-works/) has the rules for who is eligible and what happens when nobody is.

- **Capacity:** the most open conversations a teammate holds before dimmi skips them. Limits for one teammate or one channel are set through the REST API.
- **Overflow team:** the team dimmi tries when nobody in the first pool can take the conversation.
- **Queue order:** which waiting conversation goes first when a teammate frees up. **Oldest waiting first** is the default.

## Assignment status

**Assignment status** lists the pool, each teammate's open conversations and why dimmi would skip them now: **Away**, **At capacity** or **At channel capacity**. It judges teammates as for a messenger conversation.
