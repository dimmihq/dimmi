---
title: "Set up inboxes and assignment"
description: "Create separate inboxes for teams or brands, control who can see them, and turn on automatic assignment."
---

Every workspace starts with one **Support** inbox. As your team grows, you can add separate inboxes under **Settings → Inboxes** for billing, security, or different products, each with its own channels, team, and routing rules.

Each channel connection (such as one website messenger or one support email address) routes its new conversations into a single inbox.

## Core inbox settings

- **Default team:** assigns incoming threads to this team and uses its members as the pool for automatic assignment. Leave it on **No team** to pull from every active teammate.
- **Tickets:** turns every new conversation in this inbox into a [customer ticket](/tickets/how-tickets-work/) of your chosen type.
- **Access:** choose **Chosen teams and teammates** to keep sensitive queues (like HR or security) private. Owners and admins always retain access.
- **Assign to whoever replies:** turned **on** by default so the first teammate to answer takes ownership.
- **Reopen window:** how many days after closing a customer reply reopens the same thread (**30 days** by default).

Your default inbox cannot be deleted. When you delete any other inbox, dimmi asks which inbox should receive its existing conversations and channels.

## Choosing an assignment mode

Pick how dimmi hands out new conversations inside each inbox:

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual** | Leaves new threads unassigned until a teammate claims or assigns them (the default). |
| **Round robin** | Hands each new thread to the next available teammate in turn. |
| **Balanced** | Gives each new thread to the available teammate with the fewest open chats. |

For the full routing rules, see [How automatic assignment works](/inbox/how-assignment-works/).

## Capacity, overflow, and queue order

Fine-tune how automatic assignment behaves when your queue gets busy:

- **Capacity:** the maximum open conversations one teammate can hold before dimmi skips them. Individual teammate or per-channel caps can also be set via the REST API.
- **Overflow team:** a backup team dimmi checks when nobody in the main team has free capacity.
- **Queue order:** decides which waiting chat goes out next when a teammate frees up (**Oldest waiting first** by default).

## Checking who is up next

Open **Assignment status** on an inbox to see every teammate in the pool, their current open count, and why dimmi would skip them right now (**Away**, **At capacity**, or **At channel capacity**).
