---
title: "Protect conversations"
description: "Auto-redact sensitive text before storage, warn teammates about risky links, and set data retention schedules."
---

Open **Settings → Conversation safety** to turn on three workspace protections: automatic text redaction, suspicious link warnings, and retention schedules.

## Automatic redaction

Redaction replaces matching text before a message, note, or subject is saved to the database. The original value is never stored.

- **New text only:** rules apply to incoming text after you save. Past messages stay as stored, and images or file attachments are not scanned.
- **Payment card numbers:** on by default. Masks card numbers while keeping the last 4 digits.
- **US Social Security numbers:** off by default, because order and ticket IDs often match the same format.
- **Custom rules:** add up to 10 rules using Go regular expression syntax (RE2), run top to bottom after built-in rules. Default replacement text is `[redacted]`.

Use **Try your rules** to test sample text safely without saving anything.

## Link safety

Link checks run inside your teammate's browser, so changes apply to every conversation right away.

- **Warn about suspicious links:** on by default. Prompts before opening links with mismatched display text, look-alike characters, URL shorteners, or bare IP addresses.
- **Hide links in email:** off by default. Shows incoming email links as plain text; teammates with `email:read_raw` can still reveal them.
- **Allowed and blocked domains:** add up to 500 domains to either list. Allowed domains (and their subdomains) open without warnings; blocked domains cannot be opened from conversations.

## Data retention

| Item | Default | Allowed range | What happens when expired |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Closed conversations** | Kept | 30 to 3,650 days | Moves quiet closed threads to the trash. |
| **Trash** | 30 days | 7 to 365 days | Permanently deletes trashed conversations with their messages, attachments, and email records. |
| **Spam** | 30 days | 7 to 365 days | Permanently deletes spam the same way, counted from when it was marked. |
| **Original emails** | 30 days | 0 to 365 days | Deletes raw email source while keeping the parsed message. Email that never became a message (ignored, quarantined, or failed) is removed entirely. |
| **Data exports** | 7 days | 1 to 30 days | Expires download links. |

- **Daily sweep:** every retention period is applied once a day.
- **One-day buffer:** new or edited retention periods wait until the next day's run so nothing deletes the same day you save.
- **Impact preview:** the first time you set a closed conversation limit, dimmi shows how many threads are already past it.
- **Trash before permanent delete:** expired threads move to trash first, then follow your **Trash** retention window before disappearing for good.

### What dimmi cleans up on its own

Some records only exist to let someone in. Once they stop working, dimmi deletes them every night:

| Record | Deleted |
| --- | --- |
| **Anonymous Messenger sessions** | Once expired, 180 days after the visitor's last visit. |
| **Team invitations** | 30 days after they expire or are revoked. |
| **Access tokens** | 30 days after they expire or are revoked. |
| **Idempotency keys** | Once expired, 24 hours after the request. |

Permanently deleting a workspace also deletes its files: attachments, knowledge uploads, help center images, and raw email.
