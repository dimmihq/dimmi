---
title: "Protect conversations"
description: "How redaction rules, link warnings and retention periods work in dimmi, with their defaults and limits."
---

**Settings → Conversation safety** holds three protections: redaction of sensitive text, warnings on links customers send, and retention periods.

## Redaction

dimmi replaces matching text before it stores a message, a note or a subject. Nothing keeps the original.

- **Scope:** a rule acts on text that arrives after you save it. Stored messages never change, and attachments and images are not scanned.
- **Card numbers:** on by default. dimmi masks payment card numbers and keeps the last four digits.
- **US social security numbers:** off by default, because order and ticket numbers often share their shape.
- **Your rules:** up to 10, applied after the built-in rules, top to bottom.

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Pattern** | A regular expression in Go syntax (RE2), up to 256 characters. |
| **Replace with** | Up to 80 characters. The default is `[redacted]`. |

**Try your rules** shows what dimmi would store for text you paste, and stores nothing.

## Link safety

The checks run in your teammate's browser when a message shows, so a change applies to every message at once.

- **Warn about suspicious links:** on by default. dimmi asks before opening a link whose text names another site, or that uses look-alike letters, a link shortener or a bare IP address.
- **Hide links in email:** off by default. Links in incoming email show as plain text, and teammates with `email:read_raw` can show them.
- **Allowed domains:** links to these domains, and the sites under them, open without a warning.
- **Blocked domains:** links to these domains cannot be opened from a conversation.

Each list holds up to 500 domains.

## Retention

| Period | Default | Range | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Closed conversations** | Kept | 30 to 3,650 days | After that many days closed with nothing new, the conversation moves to the trash. |
| **Trash** | 30 days | 7 to 365 | dimmi empties the trash. |
| **Spam** | 30 days | 7 to 365 | dimmi deletes spam. |
| **Original emails** | 30 days | 0 to 365 | dimmi deletes the raw email. The message stays. |
| **Data exports** | 7 days | 1 to 30 | The export can no longer be downloaded. |

- **Daily run:** dimmi applies the **Closed conversations** period once a day.
- **One-day wait:** a period you set or change waits until the next day's run, so nothing is removed on the day you choose it.
- **Preview:** the first time you set the period, dimmi shows how many closed conversations are already past it.
- **Trash first:** a conversation past the period goes to the trash, where the **Trash** period then applies. After the trash empties, the conversation is gone for good.

To remove one customer's data, see [erase data](/customers/erase-data/).
