## The objects

| Object       | What it is                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Workspace    | One company's dimmi: its inboxes, customers, settings and members.                                                                               |
| Inbox        | Where conversations land, with its own team and [assignment rules](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/). A new workspace has one, named **Support**.                                    |
| Channel      | The messenger, email, Slack or the API. Each connection, such as one messenger or one support address, belongs to one inbox.                            |
| Conversation | One exchange with a customer, on any channel. Its status is open, snoozed or closed. Spam is a flag beside the status.                                                             |
| Customer     | A person who writes to your team (`contact` in the API). A user is a customer your product identified; everyone else is a lead.                |
| Teammate     | A workspace member who answers customers. A member's [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/) decides what they may do.                                                           |
| Ticket       | [Tracked work](/tickets/how-tickets-work/) with a state the customer can see. Kinds: customer ticket, back-office ticket and tracker.       |
| dimmi agent  | [The AI that answers customers](/ai/how-the-agent-works/) from your knowledge sources and hands over to a teammate. It is off in a new workspace.                                  |
| Operator     | [The AI your team chats with](/operator/work-with-operator/) inside dimmi. It proposes changes as changesets, which a teammate approves.                                           |

## How a message flows

1. A customer sends a message through a channel.
2. dimmi finds or creates the customer, then adds the message to its conversation or starts one in the inbox the channel connection belongs to.
3. If the dimmi agent is on for that channel, it answers, and hands over to a teammate when it cannot help.
4. The inbox's assignment rules give the conversation to a teammate or a team, or leave it in the **Unassigned** view.
5. A teammate replies, then closes or snoozes the conversation. A closed conversation can reopen when the customer writes again.
