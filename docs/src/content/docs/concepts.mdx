dimmi keeps support simple by separating **where messages land** (inboxes and conversations) from **the work behind them** (tickets) and **who helps** (teammates, the dimmi agent, and Operator).

Once you know how these pieces connect, every screen in dimmi makes sense.

## Main building blocks

| Concept | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Your company's home in dimmi, holding your inboxes, customers, settings, and members. |
| **Inbox** | A queue where chats arrive, with its own team and [assignment rules](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/). New workspaces start with one inbox named **Support**. |
| **Channel** | A connected source like a messenger, support email, Slack room, or the REST API. Each channel sends new chats to one inbox. |
| **Conversation** | One ongoing exchange with a customer on any channel. Every conversation is **open**, **snoozed**, or **closed**. |
| **Customer** | Anyone who writes to your team (`contact` in the API). Signed-in people your app identifies are **users**; anonymous visitors are **leads**. |
| **Teammate** | A workspace member who answers customers. Their [role](/workspace/roles-and-permissions/) controls what they can see and change. |
| **Ticket** | [Tracked work](/tickets/how-tickets-work/) behind a chat with a type, fields, and customer-facing states. Comes in three kinds: customer ticket, back-office ticket, and tracker. |
| **dimmi agent** | [The customer-facing AI](/ai/how-the-agent-works/) that replies from your docs, follows procedures, and hands off to teammates. Starts off in new workspaces. |
| **Operator** | [The internal AI](/operator/work-with-operator/) your team chats with beside a thread or in Slack. Proposes workspace edits as reviewable changesets. |

## How a message moves through dimmi

When a customer writes in, dimmi follows five steps:

1. **Matches the customer.** dimmi links the message to an existing customer record or creates a new one, then appends it to an open thread or opens a new conversation in the channel's inbox.
2. **Checks workflows and AI.** If a [workflow](/workflows/how-workflows-work/) or the [dimmi agent](/ai/how-the-agent-works/) covers that channel, it runs first and hands over when a person should help.
3. **Routes to your team.** The inbox's [assignment rules](/inbox/how-assignment-works/) give the chat to an available teammate or team, or leave it in **Unassigned**.
4. **Teammate replies.** Your team answers with customer details right next to the composer, or converts the chat into a [ticket](/tickets/how-tickets-work/) for engineering or billing.
5. **Closes or snoozes.** Close the chat when finished, or snooze it until later. If the customer writes back within the inbox's reopen window, the thread reopens.
