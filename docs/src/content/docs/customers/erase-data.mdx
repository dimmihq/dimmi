---
title: "Erase a customer's data"
description: "Permanently remove a customer's personal data and message contents, or cancel a pending erasure during the 7-day grace window."
---

import { Callout } from "~docs/components/mdx";

Open **Customers**, open the row menu for the person you want to remove, and click **Erase**. Your role must allow erasing customers (`contacts:erase`).

## What happens when you erase someone

1. **Hidden right away:** the customer disappears from your inbox and customer lists. Their email and other identities are freed so future messages create a fresh profile.
2. **Wiped after 7 days:** dimmi permanently erases every message body and attachment in their conversations, deletes the customer profile, and removes related data such as article feedback notes, changelog subscriptions, and broadcast addresses.
3. **Empty thread shells remain:** stripped conversations stay for reporting under a placeholder customer.

Check **Wipe now instead of in 7 days** to skip the grace window. dimmi asks for your password if you have not confirmed it recently.

## Cancel a pending erasure

Open the **Erasures** tab to track pending and finished requests. While a request is in **Waiting** status, click **Cancel erasure** to restore the customer and their identities.

If another customer claimed one of those identities during the wait, that identity stays with the new customer. dimmi flags it so you can [merge the two profiles](/customers/customers-and-companies/#merge-duplicates).

<Callout type="danger">Once status changes to **Erased**, the wipe is permanent and cannot be undone.</Callout>
