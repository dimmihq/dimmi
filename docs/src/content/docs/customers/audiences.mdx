---
title: "Build audiences"
description: "Create dynamic customer segments for workflows, broadcasts, series, messengers, and restricted help articles."
---

Open **Customers → Audiences** to build reusable rules that answer "who" across workflows, messenger visibility, restricted help center articles, broadcasts, and series.

Audiences are live rules, not static lists. Every time dimmi uses an audience, it evaluates the rules against the customer's current data.

## Build audience rules

- **Who:** narrow the base group to **Everyone**, **Signed-in users**, **Leads**, **Visitors**, or **Visitors and leads**.
- **Rule groups:** each group matches all, any, or none of its conditions, and **People match** sets how groups combine.
- **Conditions:** filter by customer fields, company fields, tags, custom attributes, event counts (with optional day windows), current page and device, or another audience.
- **Nesting:** audiences can reference other audiences up to 2 levels deep, with no circular references.

## Preview matches and test a person

The **Matches** column shows exact counts in workspaces up to 50,000 customers. In larger workspaces, dimmi samples around 20,000 records and labels the estimate with "About."

Use **Test a person** to check any customer against your rule. Each condition displays their actual value and **Yes**, **No**, or **Unknown**. If the rule includes page conditions and **On page** is blank, it shows **Depends on the page**.

## Freeze an audience into a tag

Because audiences update live, click **Freeze into a tag** whenever you want a fixed snapshot of everyone matching right now. Freezing is available in workspaces with up to 100,000 customers.

## Tag customers from a CSV list

Click **Upload a list** to tag existing customers from a CSV so any audience can target them.

- **Format:** CSV with a header row containing `email`, `external_id`, or `user_id` (uses the first matching column found), up to 5 MB and 50,000 rows.
- **Matches only:** tags existing customers without creating new ones, and reports unmatched values.
- **Tag limit:** customers who already have 50 tags are skipped.

## Delete an audience

dimmi blocks deleting an audience while it is still used by another audience, a live workflow, an article or collection, a messenger, a live or paused series, or a scheduled or sending broadcast.
