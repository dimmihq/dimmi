---
title: "Build audiences"
description: "How audience rules match customers, how the count is estimated, and how to freeze an audience or a CSV list into a tag."
---

An audience, built in **Customers → Audiences**, is a named rule that answers "who" for workflows, messenger settings, help center articles, broadcasts and series.

dimmi never stores who is in an audience. Each use checks the rule against the customer as they are at that moment.

## Rules

- **Who** narrows the audience to **Everyone**, **Signed-in users**, **Leads**, **Visitors** or **Visitors and leads**.
- A group matches all, any or none of its conditions. **People match** sets whether a customer needs all, any or none of the groups.
- A condition reads the customer, their company, tags, attributes, event counts, the page and device, or another audience.
- An event count can be limited to the last days you choose.
- Audiences nest 2 levels deep, with no loops.

## Matches

The **Matches** column counts every customer in a workspace of up to 50,000. Above that, dimmi reads a random sample of about 20,000 and shows the count as "About".

**Test a person** shows each condition with the customer's own value and **Yes**, **No** or **Unknown**. The result is **Depends on the page** when the rule has page conditions and **On page** is empty.

## Freeze into a tag

An audience changes as customers change. **Freeze into a tag** tags everyone who matches now, and the tag stays as it is afterward. dimmi refuses in a workspace with more than 100,000 customers.

## Upload a list

**Upload a list** tags every customer a CSV names, so a rule can use the tag.

- The file needs a header row with an `email`, `external_id` or `user_id` column. dimmi uses the first one it finds.
- At most 5 MB and 50,000 rows.
- dimmi tags only customers it has. It creates nobody and reports the values that matched no one.
- A customer who holds 50 tags is skipped.

## Delete

dimmi refuses to delete an audience that another audience, a live workflow, an article, a collection, a messenger, a live or paused series, or a scheduled or sending broadcast uses.
