dimmi keeps one record for each customer (`contact` in the API) and each company, in **Customers**.

## Where records come from

| Source | Customer | Company |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A first message on any channel | Creates a lead. | |
| An [identity token](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/) | Creates a user, or turns the lead who was chatting into one. | Finds or creates the company the token names. |
| The REST API or an [import](/customers/import/) | Finds or creates the customer. | Finds or creates the company. |
| **New contact**, **New company** | Creates one by hand. | Creates one by hand. |

## How dimmi matches a customer

1. The external ID, which is the ID your product knows the customer by.
2. A verified email address, on a record with no external ID.

A lead who only typed an email address never matches, because typing an address proves nothing. dimmi lists that lead as a possible duplicate.

dimmi matches a company by external ID, then by domain. `WWW.Acme.dev` and `acme.dev` are one company.

## Merge duplicates

To merge two records of one person, open **Merge…** in the row menu of the customer you want to keep. dimmi suggests up to 20 records with the same email, the same phone or the same name and company.

- Conversations, identities, tags and event counts move to the customer you keep.
- The kept customer's details win. The other record fills only empty fields and missing attributes.
- Links to the merged record open the kept customer.
- dimmi refuses to merge two users with different external IDs.

A lead who signs in with an identity token merges into the matching user without a teammate.

<Callout type="caution">A merge cannot be undone.</Callout>

## Notes

**Customer notes** in a conversation's details holds what your team knows about a customer, and **Notes** on a company holds the same for the company. Only your team sees them.

- **Pin:** one note per customer or company stays first. Pinning another unpins it.
- **Edit and delete:** only the teammate who wrote a note can change or delete it. Anyone can pin it.
- **Limit:** a note holds up to 10,000 characters.

## Blocked customers

The **Blocked** tab lists customers whose `blocked` field was set through the REST API. For a blocked customer:

- New conversations start as spam.
- The dimmi agent, workflows and automatic assignment skip their conversations.
- Broadcasts and series leave them out.

## Delete or erase

dimmi deletes a customer through the REST API only when the customer has no conversations. To remove a customer with a history, [erase their data](/customers/erase-data/).
