Create a workspace at [trydimmi.com/signup](https://trydimmi.com/signup). On a self-hosted server, create the first account with [`dimmictl admin`](/self-hosting/install/).

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Messengers**, select the **Website** messenger and copy its snippet. Paste it before the closing `</body>` tag of your site.

   ```html
   <script async src="https://trydimmi.com/widget/v1/loader.js" data-app-id="<app_id>"></script>
   ```

   The copied snippet has your `<app_id>` filled in.

2. Open your site and send a message from the messenger.

3. In dimmi, open **Inbox**, choose the **Unassigned** view and reply to the conversation.

</Steps>
