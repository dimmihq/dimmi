You can go from a fresh workspace to your first live reply in about **5 minutes**. Every new workspace comes with a **Support** inbox and a **Website** messenger ready to use.

On **dimmi Cloud**, your first **100 conversations** each month are free with unlimited seats. To host dimmi on your own server instead, run the [server installer](/self-hosting/install/) first, then follow the steps below.

<Steps>

1. **Create your workspace.** Sign up at [trydimmi.com/signup](https://trydimmi.com/signup) on dimmi Cloud. On a self-hosted server, run [`dimmictl admin`](/self-hosting/install/) in your terminal to create the first owner account, then sign in.

2. **Add the messenger to your site.** Open **Settings → Messengers**, pick the **Website** messenger, and copy its script tag. Paste it right before the closing `</body>` tag in your HTML:

   ```html title="index.html"
   <script async src="https://trydimmi.com/widget/v1/loader.js" data-app-id="<app_id>"></script>
   ```

   Your copied snippet already has your `<app_id>` filled in (and your own domain if you self-host).

   <Callout type="tip" title="Using React, Next.js, Vue, Angular, or Svelte?">
     Use one of our framework packages to load and control the messenger in code. See [Add the messenger to your app](/developers/messenger/install/).
   </Callout>

3. **Send a test message.** Reload your site, open the chat bubble in the corner, and send yourself a message. dimmi creates a visitor record and opens a new thread in your **Support** inbox.

4. **Reply from the inbox.** Back in dimmi, open **Inbox** and pick **Unassigned**. Click your test chat, type a reply, and send it. Your reply shows up live in the chat on your site.

</Steps>

## What to set up next

Once your first chat works, most teams add these next:

- **Identify signed-in users:** turn on [identity verification](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/) so your team sees customer details next to each chat and the dimmi agent can safely call your API.
- **Connect email or Slack:** forward your support address in [Email](/channels/support-email/), or link customer channels in [Slack](/channels/slack/).
- **Turn on the dimmi agent:** add your help center or site as a [knowledge source](/ai/knowledge-sources/), run safe tests on past chats, and [set up the dimmi agent](/ai/set-up-the-agent/).
