---
title: "Customize the messenger"
description: "Control messenger copy, languages, allowed domains, audience visibility, and linked help center tabs."
---

Edit each messenger on its page in **Settings → Messengers**. Below are the rules and behaviors behind those settings.

## Copy, languages, and launcher

- **Custom languages:** translate your greeting, away message, and labels into up to 40 languages. Any blank field falls back to **All languages**, then to dimmi's built-in text (available in 11 languages).
- **Quick links:** add up to 6 links to the messenger home screen.
- **Hidden launcher:** set **Launcher** to **Hidden** if you want to trigger chat only from [your own buttons](/channels/install-the-messenger/#open-the-messenger-from-your-own-buttons).

## Sounds

The messenger has two soft sounds: one when a message comes in, from your team or the AI agent, and one when the customer's message goes out. Both are on for new messengers. In **Sounds**, turn each on or off, and press play beside it to hear it before you save.

- **Play sounds** off silences the messenger.
- **Opening the messenger** makes no sound.
- **Browser rules:** browsers block sound until the visitor has clicked or typed on your page, so a reply that lands on a page nobody has touched arrives silently.

## Allowed sites

List up to 50 allowed origins, separated by commas. Leaving this empty allows any site to load the messenger.

```text
https://acme.example, https://*.acme.example
```

A wildcard `*.` matches one subdomain level: `https://*.acme.example` matches `https://app.acme.example` but not `https://acme.example`. Allowed sites stop unauthorized embeds; to confirm who a visitor is, turn on [identity verification](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/).

If your workspace requires signed-in customers, the messenger ignores the **Anonymous visitors** toggle.

## Control who sees the messenger

Every part of the messenger is visible to all visitors until you pick a saved [audience](/customers/audiences/).

- **Launcher audience:** hides the chat launcher from everyone outside the audience. Replies and announcements still reach them.
- **Starting a conversation:** restricts who can open a new thread. Others can still view and reply to existing threads. This audience cannot depend on values sent by a browser, such as the current page URL.

## Help and What's new tabs

Both tabs pull articles and changelog updates from the linked [help center](/help-center/set-up/). New messengers link to your default help center automatically.

If you created a messenger before creating a help center, link them by setting `help_center_id` via `PATCH /api/v1/widget-settings/{widget}`.

## Powered by dimmi badge

Control the branding badge using `show_branding` on `PATCH /api/v1/widget-settings/{widget}`. See [dimmi Cloud and self-hosted compared](/cloud-vs-self-hosted/) for when dimmi Cloud lets you hide it.
