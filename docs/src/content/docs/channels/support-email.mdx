---
title: "Connect a support email address"
description: "Forward your existing support inbox into dimmi, verify forwarding with a live test, and control how bulk mail is handled."
---

Your mail provider keeps receiving mail for your support address and forwards a copy to a dimmi forwarding address. Every forwarded email becomes a conversation in your inbox.

## Forwarding addresses

Add your support email in **Settings → Email**. dimmi assigns it a forwarding address on `inbound.trydimmi.com` (or on your own [inbound domain](/self-hosting/email/) if you self-host).

- **Default workspace address:** every new workspace starts with one address based on its slug, such as `acme@inbound.trydimmi.com`. Customers can email it right away.
- **Added addresses:** named `<slug>.<local-part>@inbound.trydimmi.com`. For example, `support@acme.example` in workspace `acme` gets `acme.support@inbound.trydimmi.com` (or `acme.support-2` if already taken).

Open any connected address to see a setup guide for your mail provider with the forwarding address filled in.

- **Inbox routing:** new addresses route into your workspace's first inbox. Change this in [Set up inboxes and assignment](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/).
- **Reply address:** replies go out from the forwarding address until you [verify your sending domain](/channels/sending-domain/).
- **Default outbound address:** new conversations started by your team send from your default address. Click **Make default** on any address to switch it.

## Confirm forwarding with your mail provider

- **Gmail and Zoho Mail:** send a verification code before forwarding starts. dimmi displays it in the **Confirmation code** row within 1 minute so you can paste it into your mail provider. dimmi never clicks confirm for you.
- **Yahoo Mail and Cloudflare Email Routing:** send a verification link instead. It arrives as a conversation in the address's inbox (closed automatically if it looks automated).

## Test your forwarding

Click **Test forwarding** to send a real test email to your support address and confirm it arrives back in dimmi.

- **Timeout and warnings:** the test fails if nothing arrives after 10 minutes, and warns you if delivery takes over 5 minutes.
- **Rate limits:** up to 1 test per address every 30 seconds, and 20 tests per workspace per day.

On a self-hosted server without outbound email configured, dimmi gives you a subject line to send manually to your support address.

## Handle bulk mail

Bulk mail includes newsletters, automated alerts, and no-reply notifications. Bulk emails belonging to an existing thread stay in that thread. For new bulk emails, your **Bulk mail** setting decides what happens:

| Option | What dimmi does |
| --- | --- |
| **Close** | Creates the conversation and closes it right away (default). |
| **Keep open** | Creates the conversation and leaves it open. |
| **Drop** | Ignores the email and creates nothing. |
