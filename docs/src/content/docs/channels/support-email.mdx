---
title: "Connect a support email address"
description: "How dimmi forwarding addresses, confirmation codes, the forwarding test and bulk mail handling work for a support address."
---

Your mail service keeps receiving mail for your support address and forwards a copy to a forwarding address in dimmi. Each forwarded email becomes a conversation.

## Forwarding addresses

Add your support address in **Settings → Email**. dimmi gives it a forwarding address on `inbound.trydimmi.com`, or on your [inbound domain](/self-hosting/email/) on a self-hosted server.

- **First address:** a new workspace has one named after its slug, such as `acme@inbound.trydimmi.com`. Customers can write to it.
- **Each address you add:** the slug, a dot and the part before the `@`. `support@acme.example` in the workspace `acme` gets `acme.support@inbound.trydimmi.com`. When that name is taken, dimmi adds a number: `acme.support-2`.

The address's page shows a setup guide for your mail service, with your forwarding address filled in.

- New addresses feed the workspace's first inbox. To change it, see [Set up inboxes and assignment](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/).
- Replies leave from the forwarding address until you [verify your domain](/channels/sending-domain/).
- Conversations your team starts leave from the default address. To change it, select **Make default** on another address.

## Confirmation codes

Gmail and Zoho Mail mail a code to a new forwarding address before they forward to it. dimmi shows the code in the **Confirmation code** row of the address's page within a minute. You enter it in your mail service: dimmi never confirms forwarding for you.

Yahoo Mail and Cloudflare Email Routing mail a link instead. It arrives as a conversation in the address's inbox, closed if it looks automated.

## Forwarding test

**Test forwarding** sends one real email to your support address and waits for it to come back through your forward.

- The test fails after 10 minutes without the email.
- dimmi warns you when the email took more than 5 minutes.
- You can run one test per address every 30 seconds, and 20 per workspace a day.

On a self-hosted server that cannot send email, dimmi shows a subject line. Send an email with that subject to your support address yourself.

## Bulk mail

Bulk mail is newsletters, notifications and mail from no-reply senders. A bulk email that belongs to an existing conversation joins it. For one that starts no thread, the address's **Bulk mail** setting decides:

| Option        | What dimmi does                                        |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Close**     | Opens a conversation and closes it. The default.       |
| **Keep open** | Opens a conversation and leaves it open.               |
| **Drop**      | Opens no conversation.                                 |
