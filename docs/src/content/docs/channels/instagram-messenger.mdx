---
title: "Connect Instagram and Messenger"
description: "How Instagram direct messages and Facebook Messenger work in dimmi: connecting an account or Page, how long you can reply, and what each message becomes."
---

Connect an Instagram account in **Settings → Integrations → Instagram**, and a Facebook Page in **Settings → Integrations → Facebook Messenger**. Each person who messages it gets a conversation in its inbox, and your replies reach them there.

- **Connect** opens Meta's sign-in. Pick your business portfolio and the accounts or Pages to share, then pick the one to connect in dimmi.
- The Instagram account must be a professional account, business or creator. Meta gives apps no access to personal accounts.
- In the Instagram app, turn on **Allow access to messages** under **Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools**. Meta sends dimmi nothing while it is off.
- You can connect several accounts and Pages. Each one feeds one inbox, which you change on its page.

## How long you can reply

Meta sets the time from the customer's last message. The composer counts it down.

| Since their last message | Who can reply |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 24 hours | Anyone, the AI agent included |
| 24 hours to 7 days | Teammates only |
| More than 7 days | Nobody, until they write again |

You cannot start a conversation. The customer writes first.

## Message requests

Instagram files a first message from someone the account does not follow under **Requests**. dimmi receives nothing from that person until you accept the request in the Instagram app.

## Messages and files

| From the customer | In dimmi |
| --- | --- |
| Text | The same text |
| Pictures and videos | Files on the message. Several sent together stay on one message. A file dimmi cannot copy, such as one over 25 MB, is named in a line. |
| A reply to your story, or a story that mentions you | A line with a link to the story. The link stops working when the story expires. |
| A shared post, reel or link | A link |
| An edited message | The new text |
| A view-once picture, and anything else Meta does not hand over | "Sent something only the Instagram app can show." |

Replies go out as plain text. dimmi sends pictures up to 8 MB and video, audio and files up to 25 MB. Instagram takes only PDF files; Messenger takes any. A reply longer than Instagram's 1,000 bytes or Messenger's 2,000 characters goes out as several messages.
