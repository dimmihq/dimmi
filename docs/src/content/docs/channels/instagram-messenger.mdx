---
title: "Connect Instagram and Messenger"
description: "Bring Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger chats into your dimmi inbox, and learn Meta's reply windows and file limits."
---

Connect an Instagram account in **Settings → Integrations → Instagram**, or a Facebook Page in **Settings → Integrations → Facebook Messenger**. Incoming messages open conversations in the channel's inbox, and your replies post back to Instagram or Messenger.

- **Connect through Meta:** click **Connect**, pick your business portfolio and the accounts or Pages to share, then choose which one to attach in dimmi.
- **Professional Instagram accounts only:** Meta only allows apps to connect business or creator accounts, not personal accounts.
- **Enable connected tools in Instagram:** open the Instagram app and turn on **Allow access to messages** under **Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools**. Without this on, Meta sends nothing to dimmi.
- **Multiple accounts:** connect multiple Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages and route each to its own inbox.

## Reply windows

Meta limits how long you can reply after a customer's last message. The composer counts down the remaining time.

| Time since customer's last message | Who can reply |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 24 hours | Teammates and the dimmi agent |
| 24 hours to 7 days | Teammates only |
| Over 7 days | Nobody, until the customer writes again |

You cannot start a new thread on Instagram or Facebook Messenger. The customer must message you first.

## Instagram message requests

When someone your Instagram account does not follow messages you for the first time, Instagram places it in **Requests**. Accept the request in the Instagram app first so Meta starts forwarding their messages to dimmi.

## Supported messages and files

| From the customer | How it appears in dimmi |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Plain text |
| Photos and videos | Attachments on the message (grouped if sent together). Files dimmi cannot download (such as over 25 MB) show their filename instead. |
| Story reply or mention | A link to the story (valid until the story expires) |
| Shared post, reel, or link | A link |
| Edited message | Updated text |
| View-once media or unsupported items | "Sent something only the Instagram app can show." |

Outbound replies send as plain text. You can attach images up to 8 MB and video, audio, or files up to 25 MB (Instagram accepts PDF files only; Messenger accepts any file). Replies longer than Instagram's 1,000-byte limit or Messenger's 2,000-character limit split automatically into multiple messages.
