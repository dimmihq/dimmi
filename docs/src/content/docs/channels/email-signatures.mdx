---
title: "Set email signatures and reply style"
description: "Which signature an email reply carries, how the Plain and Branded reply styles differ and the variables a signature can use."
---

Set an address's reply style and team signature in **Settings → Email**, and your own signature in **Settings → Profile**.

## Which signature a reply carries

dimmi uses the first signature that applies:

1. The teammate's own signature for the address the reply is from. Create it through the REST API.
2. The teammate's own signature.
3. The address's team signature.

Replies from the dimmi agent carry only the team signature. dimmi skips a signature that is turned off.

## Reply style

- **Plain:** the reply alone. The default.
- **Branded:** adds a line in your brand color above the reply and a footer of up to 500 characters below it.

## Variables

A signature holds up to 2,000 characters and no images.

- `{{member_name}}`: the teammate who replies.
- `{{address_name}}`: the address's display name.
- `{{company_name}}`: your workspace's name.

dimmi leaves out the whole line of a variable with no value. dimmi agent replies have no `{{member_name}}` line.
