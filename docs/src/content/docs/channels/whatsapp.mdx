---
title: "Connect WhatsApp"
description: "How WhatsApp works in dimmi: connecting a business number, the 24-hour window, templates, files and what Meta bills you."
---

Connect a number in **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**. Each customer who writes to it gets a conversation in the number's inbox, and your replies reach their WhatsApp.

- **Connect WhatsApp** opens Meta's signup. Pick or create your business portfolio, WhatsApp account and number there. A number you already use on the WhatsApp Business app can stay on the app.
- Meta bills your business directly for messages. Add a payment method in [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/) before you send templates.
- You can connect several numbers. Each one feeds one inbox, which you change on the number's page.

## The 24-hour window

WhatsApp accepts free text only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. The composer shows the hours left.

After 24 hours, the composer swaps the text field for your number's approved templates. Pick one, fill in its variables and check the preview before you send. The customer's reply opens a new 24-hour window.

## Templates

Teammates who can manage channels write templates on the number's page, under **New template**. Meta reviews each one before you can send it, and its answer shows beside the template.

- **Variables.** Type `{{` in the body to add one. A customer detail, such as the first name, fills in when you pick the template. You type a custom value, such as an order number, each time you send.
- **Editing.** A change sends the template back to Meta's review, and you cannot send it until Meta approves again. The name and language stay as first submitted.
- **Formatting.** The body takes bold, italic, strikethrough and monospace.

Type `/` in a reply to pick a template at any time.

Templates written in WhatsApp Manager work too. dimmi copies them when you connect a number and again whenever its copy is more than 10 minutes old. The refresh button on the number's page fetches them at once.

dimmi can send templates with a text header, a body, a footer and buttons with fixed values. It lists templates with a media header, a carousel, or buttons that need their own values, but cannot send or edit them yet.

## Messages and files

| From the customer | In dimmi |
| --- | --- |
| Text, with WhatsApp's bold, italic and strikethrough | The same text, formatted |
| Pictures, videos, voice notes and documents | Files on the message, with the caption as its text. Files over 25 MB stay out, and the message says so. |
| A shared location or contact card | A line with a map link, or the contact's name and number |
| A button or list choice | The option's title |
| A reaction | Nothing |

dimmi sends JPEG and PNG pictures up to 5 MB and MP4 videos up to 16 MB as media. It sends other files as documents, up to 100 MB. When you reply, dimmi marks the customer's latest message read.
