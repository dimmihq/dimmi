---
title: "Connect WhatsApp"
description: "Connect WhatsApp Business numbers to dimmi, reply inside the 24-hour window, send approved templates, and handle media."
---

Connect a WhatsApp Business number in **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp**. Customer messages land in that number's inbox, and your replies appear right in their WhatsApp chat.

- **Quick Meta signup:** click **Connect WhatsApp** to select or create your business portfolio, WhatsApp account, and phone number. Numbers already on the WhatsApp Business app can stay on that app.
- **Meta billing:** Meta bills your business for messages. Add a payment method in [WhatsApp Manager](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/) before sending templates.
- **Multiple numbers:** connect as many numbers as you need and assign each one to its own inbox.

## The 24-hour reply window

WhatsApp allows free-form replies only within 24 hours of a customer's last message. The inbox composer shows how many hours remain.

Once 24 hours pass, the composer switches to your approved templates. Pick a template, fill in its variables, and preview it before sending. As soon as the customer replies, a fresh 24-hour window opens.

## Message templates

Teammates with channel permissions can create templates on the number's page under **New template**. Meta reviews every template before you can send it, and its approval status displays right beside it.

- **Variables:** type `{{` in the body to add a variable. Customer fields (like first name) fill in automatically; custom values (like an order number) are entered when sending.
- **Edits trigger re-review:** editing a template sends it back to Meta for approval, and you cannot send it until approved again. Names and languages cannot change after submission.
- **Formatting:** supports bold, italic, strikethrough, and monospace.

Type `/` in the composer to insert a template at any time.

Templates created in WhatsApp Manager work too. dimmi syncs them when you connect a number and refreshes any copy older than 10 minutes (or click the refresh button to sync right away).

dimmi sends templates with a text header, body, footer, and fixed-value buttons. Templates with media headers, carousels, or dynamic buttons appear in your list but cannot be edited or sent from dimmi yet.

## Supported messages and files

| From the customer | How it appears in dimmi |
| --- | --- |
| Text (bold, italic, strikethrough) | Formatted text |
| Photos, videos, voice notes, documents | Attachments with captions as text. Files over 25 MB are skipped with a note. |
| Location or contact card | A map link, or the contact's name and phone number |
| Button or list reply | The chosen option's title |
| Emoji reaction | Ignored |

Outbound media limits: JPEG/PNG images up to 5 MB, MP4 videos up to 16 MB, and other files sent as documents up to 100 MB. Replying automatically marks the customer's latest message as read.
