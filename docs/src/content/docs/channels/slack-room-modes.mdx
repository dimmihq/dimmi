---
title: "Slack room modes"
description: "What each Slack room mode does: who counts as a customer, what becomes a conversation and how replies flow."
---

A room mode tells dimmi who the customer is in a Slack channel. Set it under **Who is the customer** in the channel's settings, on the [Slack page](/channels/slack/).

| Mode | Who is the customer | What becomes a conversation | How replies flow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shared with a customer** | People from the other organization, and guests of your Slack workspace. | Customer messages, by the channel's [trigger](/channels/slack/#triggers). | Inbox and dimmi agent replies post in the customer's thread. A reply from any member of your Slack workspace who is not a guest stops the agent, but dimmi records only linked teammates' replies. |
| **Community** | Everyone who is not a linked teammate. | Customer messages, by the trigger. | The same, but only a linked teammate's reply stops the agent. |
| **Help desk** | Your colleagues who are not linked teammates. | Colleagues' messages, by the trigger. | As in **Community**. The app's **Home** tab shows each colleague their own requests. |
| **Team only** | Nobody. | Nothing. | A linked teammate's message under a triage card becomes a note. One that starts with `>>` goes to the customer. |

- **Defaults:** a Slack Connect channel shared with another organization starts as **Shared with a customer**, and any other channel as **Community**. dimmi never changes it afterward.
- **Linked teammate:** a Slack user whose Slack email is the verified sign-in email of an active workspace member.
- **Shared channels:** when the other organization changes, dimmi unlinks the channel's company, so the new organization sees none of the first one's data.
