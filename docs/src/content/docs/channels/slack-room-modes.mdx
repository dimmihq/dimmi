---
title: "Slack room modes"
description: "Pick the right room mode for each Slack channel so dimmi knows who counts as a customer and how replies should flow."
---

A room mode tells dimmi who counts as a customer in a Slack channel. Set it under **Who is the customer** in each channel's settings on the [Slack integration page](/channels/slack/).

| Mode | Who counts as a customer | What opens a conversation | How replies flow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shared with a customer** | People from the external organization, plus Slack guests. | Customer messages matching the channel's [trigger](/channels/slack/#triggers). | Inbox and agent replies post in the customer's thread. Any non-guest member of your Slack workspace stops the agent, though only linked teammates' replies record under their name. |
| **Community** | Anyone who is not a linked dimmi teammate. | Customer messages matching the trigger. | Same flow, except only a linked teammate's reply stops the agent. |
| **Help desk** | Internal colleagues who are not linked teammates. | Colleagues' messages matching the trigger. | Same as **Community**. Each colleague sees their own requests in the app's **Home** tab. |
| **Team only** | Nobody. | Nothing. | Linked teammates' replies under a triage card save as internal notes. Messages starting with `>>` send to the customer. |

A few defaults and safeguards:

- **Smart defaults:** Slack Connect channels shared with another org start as **Shared with a customer**; all other channels start as **Community**. dimmi never changes the mode after you connect a channel.
- **Linked teammate:** any Slack user whose Slack email matches the verified sign-in email of an active workspace member.
- **Org swap protection:** if the external organization in a shared channel changes, dimmi unlinks the channel's company so the new org never sees the old org's data.
