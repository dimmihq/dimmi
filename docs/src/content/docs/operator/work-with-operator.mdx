Operator answers teammates from your workspace's data and proposes changes as [changesets](/operator/changesets/). It never writes to customers.

## Ask Operator

- **In a conversation:** open **Operator** in the conversation's header, or press `O`. The chat is about that conversation, and only you see it.
- **In Slack:** mention the dimmi app in a **Team only** channel, or send it a direct message. A link to a dimmi conversation in your first message makes the thread about it.

In Slack, Operator answers only [linked teammates](/channels/slack-room-modes/), and never in a channel customers can read.

## What Operator can do

Operator acts as you: it sees the conversations you may see and changes what your role allows.

- **Read:** conversations, customers, tags, teammates, help center articles and their drafts, procedures, the knowledge the dimmi agent answers from and reports of up to 366 days.
- **Draft:** a reply for you to copy. Operator never sends it.
- **Change:** tags, assignment, priority, status, notes, a customer's name, article drafts and procedures, all through changesets.
- **Call your systems:** [actions](/ai/actions/) with **Operator** ticked under **Used by**. Lookups run at once; changes become changesets.
- **Remember:** what you ask it to remember, up to 1,000 characters each. Only you see your memories; delete them in **Settings → Operator**.

One answer uses at most 12 model calls and 40 tool calls.

## Settings

Admins change these in **Settings → Operator**.

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operator** | On | Off stops every answer, in dimmi and in Slack. |
| **Brief conversations as they open** | Off | Operator briefs a teammate on each conversation they open. |
| **Triage new conversations** | Off | Operator proposes tags, an owner and a priority for each conversation a customer starts. |
| **What Operator may change** | **Ask first** | See [changesets](/operator/changesets/). |
| **Model** | GPT-6 Luna | A model on the dimmi agent's connection. Without GPT-6 Luna there, Operator uses the agent's model. |
| **Instructions** | Empty | Up to 8,000 characters that Operator reads with every question. |

Operator's runs count toward the dimmi agent's [monthly cap](/ai/providers/#set-the-monthly-cap), and Operator stops answering when the cap is reached. The **Operator** tab of the **Agent** page shows its runs and spend.
