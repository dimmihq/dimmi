**Operator** is an internal AI teammate built for your support team. Ask it to summarize a long chat, look up a customer's past tickets, draft a reply in your voice, or propose tags and edits as reviewable [changesets](/operator/changesets/).

Customers never see Operator, and it never sends a customer message on its own.

## Where to talk to Operator

- **Inside any conversation:** click **Operator** in the thread header, or press `O`. The chat focuses on that conversation and is private to you.
- **Inside a procedure:** click **Operator** in the procedure editor header to review recent runs that went wrong, check the playbook for gaps, or generate saved simulations.
- **In Slack:** mention the dimmi app in a **Team only** channel or send it a direct message. Paste a link to a dimmi conversation in your first message to make the Slack thread about that chat.

In Slack, Operator only replies to [linked teammates](/channels/slack-room-modes/) and stays silent in any channel customers can read.

## What Operator can do

Operator always runs with **your own role and permissions**. It only reads conversations you are allowed to open and only proposes changes your role permits.

- **Search and summarize:** reads conversations, customers, tags, teammates, help center articles and drafts, procedures, knowledge sources, and up to **366 days** of reports.
- **Draft replies:** writes a suggested reply in your workspace's tone and language, following the guidance you gave your AI agent. It checks the help center first; when that has no answer, it leans on how your team answered the same question before, and tells you which conversation.
- **Recommend with reasons:** when it suggests a teammate, a team, a tag or a priority, it says why. It looks at what your team did with similar conversations and cites them: "Assign to Maya: she handled 6 of the 8 similar conversations."
- **Propose edits:** updates tags, assignment, priority, status, notes, customer names, article drafts, procedure drafts, and saved procedure simulations through [changesets](/operator/changesets/).
- **Audit procedures:** reviews recent failed [procedure](/ai/procedure-versions/) runs and proposes working-copy fixes alongside [simulations](/ai/test-procedures/) made from the failing chats.
- **Run workspace actions:** calls [actions](/ai/actions/) where **Operator** is ticked under **Used by**. Read lookups run right away; actions that change data always wait as a changeset.
- **Remember your preferences:** tell Operator how you like summaries or drafts formatted. Your memories are private to you and can be deleted anytime in **Settings → Operator**.

## Settings and defaults

Admins manage Operator in **Settings → Operator**:

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operator** | On | Turns Operator on or off across dimmi and Slack. |
| **Brief conversations as they open** | Off | Automatically writes a private summary when a teammate opens a thread. |
| **Triage new conversations** | Off | Proposes tags, an owner, and a priority on every incoming chat. |
| **What Operator may change** | **Ask first** | Controls which changes wait for approval. See [Changesets](/operator/changesets/). |
| **Model** | GPT-6 Luna | Runs on your agent's AI connection (falls back to the agent's answer model if unavailable). |
| **Instructions** | Empty | Team guidance Operator follows on every question. |

Operator usage counts toward the dimmi agent's [monthly spend cap](/ai/providers/#set-the-monthly-cap) and stops if the cap is reached. Track Operator runs and spend on the **Operator** tab of the **Agent** page.
