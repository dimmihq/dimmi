---
title: "How tickets work"
description: "Learn the three kinds of tickets in dimmi, how ticket states work, and how tickets stay in sync with conversations."
---

A ticket tracks structured work with its own type, custom fields, and state. Every new workspace starts with four [ticket types](/tickets/ticket-types/): Question, Bug report, Back-office task, and Tracker.

## Three kinds of tickets

| Kind | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer ticket** | A customer's request, tied to one conversation. The customer can see its state and reply. |
| **Back-office ticket** | Internal work for another team, linked to a customer ticket. |
| **Tracker** | One shared issue reported by many customers. See [Trackers](/tickets/trackers/). |

## Ticket states

Every state belongs to one of five groups. Each state has an internal name for your team and a customer-facing label.

| Group | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | A new request no one has started yet. |
| **In progress** | Someone on your team is working on it. |
| **Waiting on customer** | You need a reply from the customer before moving forward. |
| **Waiting on team** | Another team or a [linked issue](/tickets/linked-issues/) is working on it. |
| **Resolved** | The work is done. |

When a ticket enters a state with **Notify** turned on, dimmi waits 2 minutes before emailing the customer. If you change the state again or a teammate replies during those 2 minutes, dimmi sends only the latest state (or skips the notice if a teammate replied).

## How customer tickets get created

- **From an open thread:** a teammate clicks **Convert to ticket** on a conversation.
- **Automatically in an inbox:** turn on auto-tickets in [inbox settings](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/) so every new conversation becomes a ticket.
- **Self-service:** customers open a ticket in the messenger or help center for any type you make visible.

## Tickets and conversations stay in sync

- **Closing resolves the ticket:** closing the conversation resolves its ticket. Any fields required to resolve must be filled first.
- **Reopening resumes work:** reopening the conversation moves the ticket back to **In progress**.
- **Customer replies move waiting tickets:** when a customer replies to a ticket in **Waiting on customer**, it moves back to **In progress**.
- **Reopen window:** replying to a resolved ticket reopens it within the type's **Reopen window** (7 days by default). After that window, dimmi opens a new follow-up ticket linked to the old one.
- **Auto-resolve:** when turned on, a ticket sitting in **Waiting on customer** resolves after your set number of days. dimmi warns the customer 1 day before.

## Escalate to another team

Click **Escalate** to create a back-office ticket for another team. The customer ticket moves to its first **Waiting on team** state while they work on it.

When the back-office ticket resolves, the customer ticket moves to **Close the loop** so a teammate can update the customer.
