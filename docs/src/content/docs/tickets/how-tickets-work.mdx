---
title: "How tickets work"
description: "The three kinds of ticket in dimmi (customer, back-office and tracker), their states, and how they connect to conversations."
---

A ticket is tracked work with a type, fields and a state. A new workspace has four [types](/tickets/ticket-types/): Question, Bug report, Back-office task and Tracker.

## Kinds

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Customer ticket | The customer's own request, tied to one conversation. The customer sees its state and can reply. |
| Back-office ticket | Internal work for another team, linked to a customer ticket. |
| Tracker | One problem many customers report. See [Trackers](/tickets/trackers/). |

## States

Each state belongs to one of five groups. A state has a name your team sees and a label the customer sees.

| Group | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Submitted** | A new request nobody has started. |
| **In progress** | Someone is working on it. |
| **Waiting on customer** | The ticket waits for the customer's answer. |
| **Waiting on team** | Another team or a [linked issue](/tickets/linked-issues/) holds it. |
| **Resolved** | Done. |

When a ticket enters a state with **Notify** on, dimmi waits 2 minutes, then tells the customer the state the ticket is in by then, unless a teammate replied meanwhile.

## Where customer tickets come from

- A teammate chooses **Convert to ticket** on a conversation.
- An inbox turns every new conversation into a ticket: see [inbox settings](/inbox/inboxes-and-assignment/).
- A customer opens one from the messenger or the help center, for types shown to customers.

## A ticket and its conversation move together

- Closing the conversation resolves the ticket. Fields required to resolve must be filled first.
- Reopening the conversation moves the ticket back to **In progress**.
- A customer's reply moves a ticket in **Waiting on customer** back to **In progress**.
- A customer's reply to a resolved ticket reopens it within the type's **Reopen window**, 7 days by default. After that, the conversation moves to a new follow-up ticket linked to the old one.
- With **Auto-resolve** on, a ticket left in **Waiting on customer** for the type's number of days resolves. dimmi tells the customer one day before.

## Escalating to another team

**Escalate** opens a back-office ticket and moves the customer ticket to its first **Waiting on team** state. When the back-office ticket resolves, the customer ticket moves to **Close the loop**, so a teammate tells the customer.
