---
title: "Link incident.io, Rootly, and PagerDuty incidents"
description: "Connect dimmi tickets to incident.io, Rootly, or PagerDuty so incident updates move tickets and responders see customer impact."
---

Connect incident.io, Rootly, or PagerDuty using an API key in **Settings → Integrations**. Once connected, incidents link to tickets and stay in sync much like [linked issues](/tickets/linked-issues/).

## Receive real-time updates via webhooks

Open your integration page and find the **Webhook address** under **Deliveries**. Add that URL as a webhook endpoint in your incident tool, paste its signing secret into **Signing secret**, and subscribe to these events:

| Tool | Events to subscribe to |
| --- | --- |
| **incident.io** | `public_incident.incident_created_v2`, `public_incident.incident_updated_v2`, `public_incident.incident_status_updated_v2` |
| **Rootly** | `incident.*` events and `incident_event.created` |

dimmi rejects incoming webhooks until you save the signing secret. As a fallback, dimmi also checks every open link every 15 minutes.

## Link or declare an incident

Click **Link issue** on any ticket to attach an existing incident or declare a new one with a severity level. Rootly also asks for a service. Declaring in PagerDuty requires a service and pages whoever is on call.

Incidents declared outside dimmi do not create tickets automatically. They only sync with tickets your team links to them.

## What dimmi writes back to the incident

When you declare an incident from dimmi, its summary includes the customer impact and a link back to the ticket. dimmi keeps those numbers up to date as more customers report the issue:

- **incident.io:** writes the affected customer count to the number custom field you pick under **Customer count field**.
- **Rootly:** writes labels starting with `dimmi_` (customers, companies, conversations, revenue, and ticket URL), leaving your other labels untouched.

Unlinking leaves those fields in place unless you check **Also remove what dimmi wrote**. If another ticket is still linked to the same incident, dimmi keeps that ticket's impact on it.

Status update messages in incident.io and timeline notes in Rootly appear as internal notes on the dimmi ticket.

## Default status mapping

Until you customize mappings under **Statuses** on the integration page, dimmi maps incident statuses like this:

| Ticket moves to | incident.io category | Rootly status | PagerDuty status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Being worked on** | `live`, `paused` | `Started`, `Mitigated` | `acknowledged` |
| **Close the loop** | `learning`, `closed` | `Resolved`, `Closed` | `resolved` |
| **Won't fix** | `declined`, `merged`, `canceled` | `Cancelled` | None |
