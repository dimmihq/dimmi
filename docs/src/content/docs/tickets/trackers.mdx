title: "Group reports with a tracker"
description: "Link every conversation reporting the same bug or outage to one tracker, measure revenue impact, and update everyone at once."
---

When several customers report the same bug or outage, link their conversations to a tracker. You see the full customer impact in one place and can message everyone back as soon as it is fixed.

## Link conversations to a tracker

On any conversation, click **Add to tracker**, then pick an open tracker or create a new one. You can also open a tracker and click **Link conversations** to add more threads.

- **Multiple trackers:** one conversation can belong to more than one tracker.
- **Live impact:** every tracker counts affected customers, companies, and total revenue at stake.
- **Dedicated view:** find all open trackers in the **Trackers** view in your sidebar.

## Update every affected customer at once

Click **Reply all** on a tracker to open a draft [conversation broadcast](/outbound/broadcasts/). Each customer gets a reply inside their own conversation thread.

By default, **Reply all** skips two groups unless you toggle the switches off:

- **Customers waiting on a reply:** so a teammate can answer their open question personally.
- **Customers already updated:** anyone a teammate replied to after the tracker's last state change.

The tracker's linked list stays locked in. You can still add an audience filter to narrow the send further.
