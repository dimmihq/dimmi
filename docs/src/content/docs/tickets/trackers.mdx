A tracker is a ticket that links every conversation reporting one problem, so you see in one place who it affects.

## Link conversations

On a conversation, choose **Add to tracker**, then pick an open tracker or start a new one. On the tracker's page, **Link conversations** adds more.

- A conversation can join several trackers.
- The tracker shows its reach: customers, companies and the revenue at stake.
- Open trackers are listed in the **Trackers** view.

## Reply to everyone

**Reply all** opens a draft [conversation broadcast](/outbound/broadcasts/) that answers every linked customer in their own conversation. It leaves out two groups until you turn the switch off:

- Customers waiting on an answer, since a person should reply to them.
- Customers a teammate answered after the tracker last changed state.

The tracker stays fixed. An audience narrows it further.
