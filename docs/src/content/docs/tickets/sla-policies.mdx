---
title: "Set SLA policies"
description: "Set response and resolution targets, decide which tickets or conversations they apply to, and choose what happens on a breach."
---

Create SLA policies in **Settings → SLA policies** to hold your team to clear response and resolution targets. See [How SLA timers work](/tickets/how-sla-timers-work/) to learn when each clock starts, pauses, and stops.

## Targets and clocks

- **Targets:** add at least one target (from 1 minute up to 1 year).
- **Warn before a breach:** alerts your team ahead of time (15 minutes by default, up to 7 days).
- **Office hours:** pick which [office hours](/inbox/office-hours/) schedule the clock follows, or choose **Around the clock (24/7)** to count all hours. New policies use your default schedule.
- **Pause while waiting on the customer:** on by default.
- **Pause while waiting on another team:** off by default.

Editing a target only affects clocks that start after you save.

## Choose what a policy applies to

| Item | How it gets a policy |
| --- | --- |
| **New ticket** | Matches the policy's **Applies to** ticket types and priorities. An empty list matches all. |
| **Conversation** | A workflow runs the **Apply an SLA** step. If the conversation has a customer ticket, the ticket gets the policy too. |

A few matching rules to know:

- **First match wins:** if several policies match a new ticket, the oldest policy applies.
- **Workflow-only policies:** leave both **Applies to** lists empty to apply the policy only through workflows.
- **Matched once:** dimmi checks policy rules once when a ticket is created.

## What happens on a breach

- **Raise priority to:** bumps priority up, but never lowers a priority that is already higher.
- **Move to team:** reassigns the team while keeping the current teammate assigned.

Need custom escalations or Slack alerts? Start a workflow with the **SLA about to breach** or **SLA breached** trigger. See [triggers and steps](/workflows/triggers-and-steps/).

## Archive a policy

Archiving a policy cancels every clock currently running under it. You cannot undo archiving a policy.
