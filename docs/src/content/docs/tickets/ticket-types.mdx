---
title: "Set up ticket types and fields"
description: "Create ticket types with their own states and custom fields, and preview how customers see them in the messenger."
---

Set up ticket types, their fields and the workspace's states in **Settings → Tickets**. A type's page shows a **Customer preview** of its form beside the settings.

## What cannot change later

- A type's kind.
- A field's **Key** and **Data type**.
- The group a state belongs to.

Archiving a type or a field keeps it, with its values, on the tickets that already use it.

## Types

- **Routing:** new tickets of the type land in the **Inbox** and **Team** you pick. By default they stay in the conversation's inbox.
- **Shown in the messenger and portal:** customers can open a ticket of this type themselves.
- **Shared with the company:** everyone at the customer's company sees the ticket, but only customers with a [verified identity](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/).
- **Reopen window:** 0 to 90 days. The default is 7.
- **Auto-resolve:** 1 to 90 days. Off by default.

## States

Each type lists the states its tickets move through, in its own order. Types can share a state.

- Removing a state from a type that still has tickets in it needs a state for them to move to. dimmi does not tell customers about that move.
- Deleting a state moves its tickets to another state of the same group.

## Fields

A field has one of 12 data types: short text, long text, number, decimal, currency, yes or no, date, single choice, multiple choice, file, teammate and company.

- A field can be required to create a ticket, apart for teammates and for customers, so the customer's form can ask less.
- A field required to resolve applies to everyone: the inbox, the API, the dimmi agent and workflows. It also blocks closing the ticket's conversation.
- **Show** hides a field until another field is filled, or holds one of the answers you pick. A hidden field is never required.
- **How the AI fills it** tells dimmi what to look for in the conversation. dimmi suggests a value, and writes it without a teammate only when the field or its type fills automatically.
