---
title: "Set up ticket types and fields"
description: "Create ticket types with custom states and fields, and preview how the form looks to customers."
---

Manage ticket types, fields, and states in **Settings → Tickets**. Every ticket type page shows a **Customer preview** right beside the form settings so you can check how customers see it.

## Settings you cannot change later

Choose these carefully when creating a type, field, or state:

- A ticket type's kind (customer, back-office, or tracker).
- A custom field's **Key** and **Data type**.
- The group a state belongs to.

If you no longer need a type or field, archive it. Existing tickets keep their saved values.

## Ticket type settings

- **Routing:** send new tickets of this type to a specific **Inbox** and **Team**. By default, they stay in the conversation's inbox.
- **Shown in the messenger and portal:** lets customers submit this ticket type on their own.
- **Shared with the company:** lets everyone at the customer's company view the ticket. This only works for customers with a [verified identity](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/).
- **Reopen window:** how many days a customer reply reopens a resolved ticket (0 to 90 days; 7 by default).
- **Auto-resolve:** close tickets left in **Waiting on customer** after 1 to 90 days. Off by default.

## States

Each ticket type lists its own states in order, and multiple types can share the same state.

- **Removing a state from a type:** if tickets still use that state, pick a replacement state to move them to. Customers are not notified about this move.
- **Deleting a state:** moves its tickets to another state in the same group.

## Custom fields

dimmi supports 12 field types: short text, long text, number, decimal, currency, yes or no, date, single choice, multiple choice, file, teammate, and company.

- **Required to create:** set separate rules for teammates and customers, so customer forms stay short.
- **Required to resolve:** enforced everywhere (inbox, API, dimmi agent, and workflows) and blocks closing the conversation until filled.
- **Conditional visibility:** use **Show** to hide a field until another field has a value or matches a specific choice. Hidden fields are never required.
- **AI autofill:** fill in **How the AI fills it** to tell dimmi what to extract from the conversation. By default dimmi suggests a value; it only saves automatically when you turn on autofill for that field or type.
