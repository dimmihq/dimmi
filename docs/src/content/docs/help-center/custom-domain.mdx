---
title: "Use a custom domain for your help center"
description: "The DNS records that put your help center on your own domain, how dimmi checks them and when the domain stops working."
---

Add a subdomain you own, such as `help.acme.example`, under **Custom domain** in **Settings → Help centers**, then create two DNS records.

A bare domain such as `acme.example` cannot be used, because it cannot carry a `CNAME` record.

## DNS records

The **Custom domain** section shows both records with values to copy.

| Record | Name | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | `help.acme.example` | `custom.dimmi.help` on dimmi Cloud | Points your domain at dimmi. |
| `TXT` | `_dimmi.help.acme.example` | `dimmi-verification=<token>` | Proves the domain is yours. |

On a self-hosted server the `CNAME` value is your server's own target. See [help center and custom domains](/self-hosting/domains/).

## Check schedule

dimmi checks a new domain within a minute. While a record is missing, it checks again after 1, 2, 5, 10 and 30 minutes, then every hour.

After 72 hours without both records, the domain is **Failed**. **Check now** starts a new 72-hour window.

## When the domain is live

- **Address:** the help center answers at your domain over HTTPS, with a certificate dimmi issues for it.
- **Links:** the help center's links and canonical addresses use your domain.
- **Old address:** the `dimmi.help` address keeps working.

## Losing the records

dimmi checks a **Live** domain once a day.

When a check fails, the domain keeps serving and dimmi follows the check schedule again. After 72 hours without both records, the domain is **Failed** and the help center answers only at its old address.

## Rules

- A help center has one custom domain. Remove it before you add another.
- A domain serves one help center.
- After **Remove**, the domain stops answering within 30 seconds.
