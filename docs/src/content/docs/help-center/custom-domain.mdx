---
title: "Use a custom domain for your help center"
description: "Point a subdomain like help.example.com at your dimmi help center with two DNS records and automatic HTTPS."
---

Open **Settings → Help centers**, enter a subdomain you own (such as `help.acme.example`) under **Custom domain**, and add two DNS records at your DNS provider.

You must use a subdomain. Apex domains like `acme.example` cannot hold a `CNAME` record.

## DNS records to add

Copy the exact values shown under **Custom domain**:

| Record | Name | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CNAME` | `help.acme.example` | `custom.dimmi.help` (on dimmi Cloud) | Routes traffic to dimmi. |
| `TXT` | `_dimmi.help.acme.example` | `dimmi-verification=<token>` | Proves you own the domain. |

On a self-hosted server, the `CNAME` points to your own server target. See [Help center and custom domains](/self-hosting/domains/).

## How verification works

dimmi checks your new domain within 1 minute. If a record is not live yet, dimmi retries after 1, 2, 5, 10, and 30 minutes, then once every hour.

If both records are still missing after 72 hours, the domain moves to **Failed**. Click **Check now** to start a fresh 72-hour window.

## Once your domain is live

- **Automatic HTTPS:** dimmi issues and renews an SSL certificate for your domain.
- **Canonical URLs:** links and canonical tags across the help center switch to your custom domain.
- **Fallback address:** your original `dimmi.help` URL keeps working.

## If DNS records break later

dimmi rechecks **Live** domains once a day. If a check fails, your domain stays up while dimmi retries on the same schedule. If both records stay missing for 72 hours, the domain becomes **Failed** and the help center falls back to its original address.

## Limits

- Each help center supports one custom domain. Remove the current one before adding another.
- One custom domain can only point to one help center.
- After clicking **Remove**, the domain stops answering within 30 seconds.
