---
title: "Limit articles to some customers"
description: "Restrict help center articles or collections to a saved audience and let readers sign in to view them."
---

To show an article only to specific customers (such as enterprise plans or signed-in users), open **Article settings** and pick a saved [audience](/customers/audiences/) under **Audience**.

## What restricting an article does

- **Hidden from others:** people outside the audience cannot see the article in listings or search results.
- **Protected in AI replies:** the dimmi agent never quotes or answers from an article the current customer is not allowed to read.
- **Instant update:** audience changes take effect right away without republishing.
- **Restrict a whole collection:** call `PATCH /api/v1/collections/{collection}/audience`.

dimmi blocks audiences that rely on values sent by a browser (such as page URLs). Access rules can only use trusted data your backend sent to dimmi.

## How readers sign in

The help center shows a **Sign in** button whenever restricted content exists or you set a product login URL. Configure sign-in options under **Readers sign in with** in **Settings → Help centers**.

| Sign-in method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Email code** | On by default. Readers enter their email and a 6-digit code. Codes last 10 minutes and expire after 5 wrong guesses. |
| **Your product's login** | Sends readers to your app to sign in, then bounces them back with a signed identity token. |
| **Messenger** | Customers already identified in the messenger open help articles signed in automatically. |

A few limits to note:

- **Email code rate limits:** 1 code every 30 seconds, up to 5 per hour per email address.
- **Matched customer:** signing in with a code logs the reader in as the matching customer, or creates a new lead.
- **Session length:** readers stay signed in for 7 days after their last visit, scoped to that help center.

## Sign readers in through your app

1. Set **Your product's login** to an `https` URL in your app, such as `https://app.acme.example/help-login`.
2. When a reader clicks **Sign in**, dimmi redirects them to that page with a `return_to` query parameter.
3. Sign the user in on your side, then create an [identity token](/developers/messenger/identity-verification/) on your server.
4. Redirect back to `return_to` with `#token=<identity_token>` appended:

```text
https://acme.dimmi.help/sso?next=%2Farticles%2Fbilling#token=<identity_token>
```

Always check that `return_to` starts with your help center's origin before redirecting.

If your token includes an `aud` claim, it must match `dimmi`, `dimmi-help`, or your workspace ID. Tokens with a `nonce` claim can only be used once.
