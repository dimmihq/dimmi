title: "Set up a help center"
description: "Where your help center lives, how collections and articles publish, and the defaults you can change."
---

A new workspace already has one help center, named after the workspace. Change it in **Settings → Help centers** and write articles under **Help center** in the sidebar.

## Address

- **dimmi Cloud:** `https://<subdomain>.dimmi.help`.
- **Self-hosted:** `/hc/<subdomain>` on your server, or a subdomain of your [help domain](/self-hosting/domains/).
- **Subdomain:** 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and dashes, unique across every workspace.
- **Your own domain:** see [custom domain](/help-center/custom-domain/).

## Several help centers

**New help center** adds another one, such as one per product or language, with its own address, articles and settings.

- **Languages:** English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Dutch and Japanese.
- **Default:** the first help center is the default and cannot be deleted. New messengers show it.
- **Deleting:** only an Owner can delete a help center. Its articles go with it, and messengers that showed it show the default instead.
- **dimmi agent:** it answers from the published articles of every help center.

## Collections and articles

- **Depth:** collections nest three levels deep.
- **Deleting a collection:** its articles stay, without a collection.
- **Publishing:** saving an article changes nothing for readers. **Publish** makes the saved text live and keeps a version you can restore.
- **Who can read it:** **Public** articles show on the help center. **Agent only** articles stay hidden, and only the dimmi agent reads them.
- **Addresses:** when you change the address of a published article or a collection, the old address redirects to the new one.

To show an article only to some customers, see [restricted articles](/help-center/limit-articles/).

## Defaults

| Setting | Default | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand color** | `#10b981` | A hex color. |
| **Home title** | "How can we help?" | 80 characters. dimmi cuts longer text. |
| **Home subtitle** | Empty | 200 characters. |
| **Footer** | Empty | 500 characters. |
| **Contact link** | None | An `https` address. |
| **Show the messenger** | On, with your first messenger | One messenger per help center. |
| **Ask AI** | On | |
| **Search engines** | On | |

Reader search covers published **Public** articles the reader may read.
