---
title: "Set up a help center"
description: "Configure your help center address, organize collections and articles, and run multiple help centers for different products."
---

Every new workspace comes with a help center ready to go. Change its settings in **Settings → Help centers**, and write articles under **Help center** in the sidebar.

## Your help center address

- **dimmi Cloud:** `https://<subdomain>.dimmi.help`.
- **Self-hosted:** `/hc/<subdomain>` on your server, or a subdomain of your [help domain](/self-hosting/domains/).
- **Subdomain format:** 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits, and dashes, unique across all workspaces.
- **Custom domain:** put your help center on your own domain with [Use a custom domain](/help-center/custom-domain/).

## Run multiple help centers

Click **New help center** to spin up another help center for a second product. Each one has its own address, articles, and settings.

- **Languages:** one help center serves many languages. Turn them on and translate articles with [Translate your help center](/help-center/languages/).
- **Default help center:** your first help center is the default and cannot be deleted. New messengers show it automatically.
- **Deleting:** only an Owner can delete a help center. Deleting removes its articles and switches any messengers using it back to the default.
- **dimmi agent:** answers from published articles across all of your help centers.

## Collections and articles

- **Nesting:** collections can nest up to 3 levels deep. Deleting a collection leaves its articles intact without a collection.
- **Drafts and publishing:** saving edits keeps them as a draft. Click **Publish** to make them live and save a version you can restore later.
- **Visibility:** **Public** articles show on the help center. **Agent only** articles stay hidden from readers and are read only by the dimmi agent.
- **Automatic redirects:** renaming the URL slug of a published article or collection redirects the old link to the new one automatically.

Want to hide an article from everyone except specific customers? See [Limit articles to some customers](/help-center/limit-articles/).

## Default settings

| Setting | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand color** | `#10b981` | Any hex color. |
| **Home title** | "How can we help?" | Shown at the top of your help center. |
| **Home subtitle** | Empty | Short intro line under the title. |
| **Footer** | Empty | Shown at the bottom of every page. |
| **Contact link** | None | Any `https` URL. |
| **Show the messenger** | On (uses your first messenger) | Link one messenger per help center. |
| **Ask AI** | On | Lets readers ask questions in the help center. |
| **Search engines** | On | Allows search engines to index public articles. |

Reader search covers all published **Public** articles the visitor has permission to view.

Your help center header adds links to your [changelog](/help-center/changelog/) once you publish an entry, and to [feature requests](/help-center/feature-requests/) once you open the board to customers.
