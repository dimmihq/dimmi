---
title: "Publish a changelog"
description: "Publish product updates to your help center, messenger, RSS feed, and email subscribers."
---

Write product updates under **Changelog** in the sidebar. Every published entry appears in three places at once:

- **Help center:** live at `/changelog` and linked from your header as soon as you publish your first entry. Your home page highlights the 3 newest entries.
- **Messenger:** shown inside the **What's new** tab.
- **RSS feeds:** available at `/changelog/feed.xml`, or per label at `/changelog/labels/<label>/feed.xml`.

## Write and publish entries

Saving edits keeps an entry as a draft. Click **Publish** to push it live right away, or **Schedule** to publish at a future time.

- **Backdate releases:** after publishing, set a past **Shipped on** date in **Update settings** for earlier releases.
- **Authors:** every teammate who saves an entry is listed as an author by default, or use **Pick authors** to choose up to 5.
- **Labels:** starts with **New**, **Improved**, and **Fixed** (up to 20 labels total). Deleting a label keeps its entries intact.

## Email subscribers

Check **Email N subscribers** when publishing to notify readers by email. dimmi sends each entry to each confirmed subscriber only once, even if you republish edits later.

| Setting in **Settings → Changelog** | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subscriptions** | On | Lets readers subscribe with their email on `/changelog`. |
| **What the email holds** | **The full entry** | Switch to **A summary** to send the summary with a link to read more. |
| **Undo window** | **None** | Wait 5 minutes, 15 minutes, or 1 hour before emailing. Unpublish inside that window to cancel the send. |

- **Double opt-in:** visitors who type an email must confirm via a link before receiving updates. Readers already signed in to your help center subscribe right away.
- **Resend throttle:** dimmi sends at most 1 confirmation email per address every 10 minutes.
- **One-click unsubscribe:** every email includes a personal unsubscribe link.

## Reactions and analytics

Readers can react with thumbs up, heart, party popper, rocket, or eyes. Each reader counts once per emoji on an entry.

Open **Analytics** under **Changelog** to view stats for the last 7, 30, or 90 days:

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Views** | Page opens, excluding crawlers and RSS feeds. |
| **Reactions** | Reactions left during the period. |
| **Subscribers** | Current confirmed subscribers, plus new signups during the period. |
| **Unsubscribed** | Subscribers who left during the period. |

dimmi keeps daily totals for 400 days and does not track individual readers.

Publishing, updating, or unpublishing an entry sends `changelog_entry.published`, `changelog_entry.updated`, and `changelog_entry.unpublished` [webhook events](/developers/webhooks/events/).
