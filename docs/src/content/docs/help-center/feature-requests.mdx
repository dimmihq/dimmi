---
title: "Run a feature requests board"
description: "Publish a public or private feature request board on your help center, manage votes and statuses, and notify voters when you ship."
---

Open **Settings → Feature requests**, turn on **Open to customers**, and save. Your board goes live at `/feedback` on your help center. It starts turned off in a new workspace.

Customers sign in to post, vote, and comment using the same sign-in flow as [restricted articles](/help-center/limit-articles/#how-readers-sign-in).

## Set up boards

Every help center starts with one board. Add more in **Settings → Feature requests** if you want separate spaces for feature ideas and bug reports.

| Visibility | Who can view and post |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone can read. Signed-in customers can post and vote. |
| **Signed-in customers** | Only signed-in customers can see the board. |
| **Internal** | Only your team can see it. |

Turn on **Approve customers' posts before they show** to hold new submissions in a queue until a teammate approves them. Authors can still see their own pending posts.

## Statuses and voter updates

Statuses are shared across your workspace. Five come ready out of the box, and you can rename, recolor, reorder, add, or delete them.

| Default status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Under consideration** | **Open.** New requests land in the first open status. |
| **Planned** | **Planned.** |
| **In progress** | **In progress.** |
| **Shipped** | **Done.** |
| **Closed** | **Closed.** Leaves the roadmap while keeping its page live. |

When you change a request's status, **Email N voters and whoever posted it** is checked by default. dimmi emails the author, every voter, and customers from linked conversations once, along with your **Note**.

When moving a request to a **Done** status, pick a **Changelog update** to link the [changelog](/help-center/changelog/) entry that shipped it. The voter email includes a link to that entry.

## Votes and linking conversations

- **One vote per customer:** each customer can vote once on a request.
- **Vote on a customer's behalf:** teammates can add a vote for a customer and record **How much it matters to them**.
- **Link from the inbox:** click **Link to feature request** inside a conversation to connect it and add a vote for that customer.
- **Link from the board:** click **Link a conversation** on a request to search by text, customer, or number and attach it.
- **Trending sort:** recent votes carry double weight for each week newer they are, pushing fresh demand to the top.

## Comments, duplicates, and webhooks

- **Comments:** customers and teammates can reply one level deep. Internal notes stay visible only to your team.
- **Merge duplicates:** merging moves all votes, comments, and linked conversations onto the request you keep, deduplicates voters, and redirects the old URL.
- **Deleting:** deleting a request, board, or status does not email anyone.

dimmi emits `feedback_post.*` [webhook events](/developers/webhooks/events/) when requests are created, voted on, updated, merged, or linked to conversations.
