---
title: "Send a broadcast"
description: "Send one-time updates over email, messenger, Slack, or active conversations, with smart safeguards and reply routing."
---

Create one-time announcements in **Broadcasts**. Pick your channel when creating the broadcast (you cannot switch channels later), pick your audience, and send or schedule it.

## Broadcast channels

| Channel | Who it reaches | How it arrives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | Customers with an email address. | Individual emails from a connected address on your [verified sending domain](/channels/sending-domain/). dimmi never sends broadcasts from its own domain. |
| **Chat** | Customers who have used the messenger. | A message from the teammate in **Sent as**. Shows right away if open, or waits unread. |
| **Post** | Customers who have used the messenger. | Same as Chat, displayed as a titled card. |
| **Slack** | All customer [Slack channels](/channels/slack/), or specific channels you pick. | One post per channel, sent as the dimmi app or the teammate's Slack user. |
| **Conversation** | Each customer's latest thread, or every thread linked to a [tracker](/tickets/trackers/). | A reply from **Sent as** inside that thread, on whatever channel it uses. |

## Who receives it

dimmi locks the recipient list the moment sending begins. Customers who match your [audience](/customers/audiences/) afterward do not receive it.

By default, dimmi skips:

- Blocked customers, or anyone unreachable on that channel.
- Email addresses that previously bounced or marked you as spam.
- Duplicate customer profiles sharing the same email address.
- Customers who unsubscribed globally or from the broadcast's [topic](/outbound/topics/).
- Customers who received another broadcast in the last 72 hours (unless you enable **Ignore the frequency cap**).

Marking a broadcast as an **Important notice** bypasses unsubscribes and the 72-hour cap, though it still skips bounced and spam-flagged addresses.

Conversation broadcasts skip these filters and reply once per customer. You can optionally exclude customers still waiting on a reply, or (for trackers) anyone a teammate updated since the last tracker state change.

The preview step counts up to 50,000 recipients. After sending, the broadcast report lists every skipped customer and why.

## Personalize with variables

```text
Hi {{contact.first_name | default: "there"}}, a note from {{sender.first_name}} at {{workspace.name}}.
```

Available variables include `contact.name`, `contact.first_name`, `contact.email`, `company.name`, `sender.name`, `sender.first_name`, and `workspace.name`, plus any custom customer or company [attribute](/customers/attributes-and-events/) via `contact.<key>` and `company.<key>`.

## Test and schedule

- **Send test:** available for email and Slack. Uses your own profile details, prefixes `[Test]` to the subject, and stays out of reports.
- **Local time zone delivery:** turn on **In each person's time zone** to deliver at the chosen wall-clock time for each customer. Anyone whose local time already passed receives it right away.

## Email warm-up and automatic pause

- **Warm-up:** a new broadcast sending address starts at 500 emails on day 1 and doubles each day until it reaches 50,000/day. Extra recipients queue for the next day.
- **Bounce protection:** after 100 emails sent, dimmi pauses the broadcast automatically if bounce rates pass 5% or spam complaints pass 0.3%.

## Where replies go

When a customer replies over email, messenger, or Slack, dimmi opens a conversation starting with your broadcast and routes it to the **Team** and **Assigned to** settings you picked. The dimmi agent only answers if **The agent answers replies** is enabled.

Messenger broadcasts start out closed so they do not clutter your inbox until a customer replies. Conversation broadcasts post inside the existing thread without creating a new one.
