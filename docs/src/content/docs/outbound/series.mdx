---
title: "Build a series"
description: "Automate multi-step onboarding and lifecycle messages with waits, branches, split tests, and safe daily caps."
---

Open **Series** to build automated journeys that guide customers through messages, waits, and checks on their own schedule.

Switch **Who travels it** to **Accounts** if you want an entire company to travel the series once instead of individual contacts.

## How customers enter a series

- **Rule or audience match:** choose **They match rules** or **They join a saved audience**. Only people who start matching after launch enter, unless you enable **People already matching**.
- **Event trigger:** choose **They do an event** to start a journey when dimmi receives a tracked [event](/developers/messenger/track-events/).
- **Manual or API:** a teammate or your backend adds customers to the series.

dimmi evaluates profile changes within seconds, and rechecks time-based rules (like a trial ending in 5 days) every 15 minutes.

Each customer travels a series once unless **Can travel it again** is turned on, and never twice at the same time. Blocked customers and anyone already matching **Leave early** never enter.

## Available steps

A series supports up to 200 steps, with up to 8 outgoing paths per step.

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email**, **Chat**, **Post**, **Slack** | Sends a message with the same reach checks, pacing, tracking, and reply routing as a [broadcast](/outbound/broadcasts/). |
| **Wait** | Pauses up to 365 days, until a local time of day, or relative to a date attribute like `trial_ends_at`. Dates in the past advance right away. |
| **Wait for** | Pauses until an event fires, rules match, or the customer opens, clicks, or replies to an earlier message. Takes the timeout path if time runs out. |
| **Check** | Follows the first matching branch, or the fallback branch. |
| **Split test** | Permanently assigns each customer to 1 of 2 to 4 variants by percentage. |
| **AI path** | Uses AI to read the customer, company, and recent threads and pick a path. Falls back after 20 seconds or on error. |
| **Tag**, **Remove tag**, **Set attribute**, **Topic** | Updates the customer (or company in an account series). |
| **Webhook** | Sends the customer and step payload to your endpoint (does not follow redirects). See [Workflow requests](/developers/workflow-requests/). |

## Sending limits

- **Daily cap:** each customer receives at most 2 series messages per day across all series. Extra messages wait until the next day.
- **Quiet hours:** **Email only by day** holds emails until 8:00 to 20:00 in the customer's local time zone.

## Exit rules and goals

Before every step, dimmi evaluates your **Leave early** rules against the customer's current state. Anyone who exits stops receiving queued steps.

The **Goal** window begins the moment the customer enters the series.

## Publish, pause, and archive

- **Publish changes:** customers already in the series pick up the new version from their current step. If their step was deleted, they move to the next remaining step.
- **Pause:** halts all sends and movement. Waits that finish during a pause resume once you click **Resume**.
- **Archive:** ends all active journeys permanently. Archived series cannot be republished.

## Test before launching

Click **Try it on me** to run the published version using the customer profile with your own email address. Real messages send to you, caps and quiet hours are skipped, you can fast-forward through waits, and test runs stay out of reports.
