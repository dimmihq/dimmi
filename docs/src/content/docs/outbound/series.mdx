---
title: "Build a series"
description: "Automate multi-step onboarding and lifecycle messages with waits, branches, split tests, and safe daily caps."
---

Open **Series** to build automated journeys that guide customers through messages, waits, and checks on their own schedule.

Switch **Who travels it** to **Accounts** if you want an entire company to travel the series once instead of individual contacts.

## How customers enter a series

- **Rule or audience match:** choose **They match rules** or **They join a saved audience**. Only people who start matching after launch enter, unless you enable **People already matching**.
- **Event trigger:** choose **They do an event** to start a journey when dimmi receives a tracked [event](/developers/messenger/track-events/).
- **Manual or API:** a teammate or your backend adds customers to the series.

dimmi evaluates profile changes within seconds, and rechecks time-based rules (like a trial ending in 5 days) every 15 minutes.

Each customer travels a series once unless **Can travel it again** is turned on, and never twice at the same time. Blocked customers and anyone already matching **Leave early** never enter.

## Available steps

A series supports up to 200 steps, with up to 8 outgoing paths per step.

| Step | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Email**, **Chat**, **Post**, **Slack** | Sends a message with the same reach checks, pacing, tracking, and reply routing as a [broadcast](/outbound/broadcasts/). |
| **Wait** | Pauses up to 365 days, until a local time of day, or relative to a date attribute like `trial_ends_at`. Dates in the past advance right away. |
| **Wait for** | Pauses until an event fires, rules match, or the customer opens, clicks, or replies to an earlier message. Takes the timeout path if time runs out. |
| **Check** | Follows the first matching branch, or the fallback branch. |
| **Split test** | Permanently assigns each customer to 1 of 2 to 4 variants by percentage. |
| **AI path** | Uses AI to read the customer, their company and recent events and pick a path. Falls back after 20 seconds or on error. |
| **Extract with AI** | Uses AI to read the same profile and write typed values to the customer's attributes. A value the AI isn't sure of takes its fallback, or the **Not found** path. |
| **Tag**, **Remove tag**, **Set attribute**, **Topic** | Updates the customer (or company in an account series). |
| **Run an action** | Calls one of your published [actions](/ai/actions/) with inputs from the customer's details and attributes, and writes the outputs you pick to their attributes. Takes the **Action failed** path on an error. Series made earlier may still hold a **Webhook** step, which keeps running. |

## Sending limits

- **Daily cap:** each customer receives at most 2 series messages per day across all series. Extra messages wait until the next day.
- **Quiet hours:** **Email only by day** holds emails until 8:00 to 20:00 in the customer's local time zone.

## Replies

A reply to a series message opens a conversation in the inbox, routed to the **Replies go to** teammate or team on the entry step. An AI agent answers it only when **An AI agent answers replies** is on. **Answered by** picks the agent; left empty, the agent placed on the channel the reply comes in on answers.

## Draft it with Operator

On **Series**, click **Describe it** and say who it is for and what each step sends. [Operator](/operator/work-with-operator/) drafts the whole series, messages included. **Create draft** opens the builder, and nothing is sent until you publish.

Inside the builder, open **Operator** to add a follow-up, rewrite a message or change the goal. Its changes show in the builder for you to accept.

## Exit rules and goals

Before every step, dimmi evaluates your **Leave early** rules against the customer's current state. Anyone who exits stops receiving queued steps.

The **Goal** window begins the moment the customer enters the series.

### Control group

A goal rate alone can't tell you whether the series caused it. Set **Control group, percent** (0 to 50) to hold that share of people out: they enter, receive nothing, and leave at once, and dimmi still counts their goal for the whole window.

The entry step's results then show both groups side by side. For example, with a 10% control group:

| Group | People | Reached the goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Got the series | 900 | 27% |
| Held out | 100 | 19% |
| **Lift** | | **+8 pts** |

Who is held out is decided once, when they enter. Test journeys never join the control group.

## Publish, pause, and archive

- **Publish changes:** customers already in the series pick up the new version from their current step. If their step was deleted, they move to the next remaining step.
- **Pause:** halts all sends and movement. Waits that finish during a pause resume once you click **Resume**. Both sit in the series' **⋯** menu, beside **Archive**.
- **Archive:** ends all active journeys permanently. Archived series cannot be republished.

## Test before launching

Open the testing panel from the header, then click **Try it on me** under **Preview** to run the published version using the customer profile with your own email address. Real messages send to you, caps and quiet hours are skipped, you can fast-forward through waits, and test runs stay out of reports.

To check the paths without sending anything, open **Simulations** in the same panel and walk one person through the draft or the live version. Pick a contact or describe a made-up person, and say whether they open, click or reply, and which events they do. Every wait is skipped, and you see each step with the path it took, every message that would go out, the changes that would be made, and whether they reach the goal. AI paths still ask the AI, so they pick the way a real journey would.
