---
title: "Manage topics and subscriptions"
description: "How topics, the preferences page and unsubscribe links decide who receives a broadcast or series email."
---

A topic is a list customers can leave without leaving every broadcast.

Manage topics under **Subscriptions → Topics** in **Broadcasts**, then set one as the **Subscription topic** of a broadcast or the **Topic** of a series.

## Opt in or opt out

| Consent | Who receives the topic |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone, until they opt out** | Every customer who has not left it. |
| **Only people who opt in** | Only customers who chose it. |

You set consent when you create a topic, and it cannot change afterward. A name holds up to 80 characters. **Archive** keeps every choice customers made.

## What the counts mean

| Column | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscribed** | The people the topic reaches now. For an opt-out topic, that is everyone who has not left it or left all broadcasts. |
| **Unsubscribed** | The people who left this topic. |
| **Broadcasts** | The broadcasts that use this topic. |

## How customers unsubscribe

Every broadcast email ends with a footer that links to a preferences page. The page needs no sign-in, because the link is signed for that one recipient.

- **Preferences page:** the customer switches each topic on or off, or picks **Unsubscribe from all**.
- **One-click unsubscribe:** leaves the email's topic, or every broadcast when the email has no topic.
- **Series:** a **Topic** step subscribes or unsubscribes a customer as they pass it.

dimmi honors an unsubscribe at once. It does not stop a broadcast marked **Important notice**, a series with **Service messages** on, or the replies in a conversation.
