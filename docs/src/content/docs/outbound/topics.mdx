---
title: "Manage topics and subscriptions"
description: "Let customers opt out of specific broadcast or series topics without unsubscribing from everything."
---

A topic is a subscription category (like Product updates or Security notices) so customers can opt out of one kind of email without muting everything you send.

Create topics under **Broadcasts → Subscriptions → Topics**, then attach a topic to any broadcast or series.

## Opt-out vs. opt-in topics

| Consent mode | Who receives emails on this topic |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone, until they opt out** | Every reachable customer who has not unsubscribed. |
| **Only people who opt in** | Only customers who explicitly opted in. |

You pick the consent mode when creating a topic and cannot change it later. Archiving a topic preserves all customer subscription choices.

## Understanding topic counts

| Column | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Subscribed** | Customers reachable on this topic right now. For opt-out topics, that is everyone who has not left this topic or unsubscribed from all broadcasts. |
| **Unsubscribed** | Customers who opted out of this topic. |
| **Broadcasts** | Broadcasts linked to this topic. |

## How customers manage preferences

Every broadcast email includes a footer link to a personal preferences page. No sign-in is required because each link is signed for that recipient.

- **Preferences page:** customers toggle individual topics on or off, or click **Unsubscribe from all**.
- **One-click unsubscribe:** opts the customer out of the email's topic (or all broadcasts if no topic was set).
- **Series step:** use a **Topic** step in a series to subscribe or unsubscribe customers automatically.

Unsubscribes take effect right away. They never block broadcasts marked **Important notice**, series with **Service messages** enabled, or normal support conversation replies.
