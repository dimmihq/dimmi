Saved replies are answers your team writes once and inserts with a shortcut. Type `/` in the composer, start typing the reply's name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

Saved replies can include the customer's first name with `{{first_name}}`, and you can edit the text before you send it, so they never have to sound canned.
