---
title: Cancel your subscription
description: Cancel at any time from billing settings; your data stays available until the end of the period.
collection: billing
author: ama
updated: 2026-09-15
---
Owners can cancel from **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Your workspace stays on its plan until the end of the period you've paid for, then moves to the Free plan.

Nothing is deleted when you cancel. Export your conversations and contacts from **Settings → Data** whenever you like.
