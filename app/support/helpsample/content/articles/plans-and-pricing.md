Every plan includes the shared inbox, the messenger, the help center and the changelog. Plans differ in how many teammates and help centers you can have.

| | Free | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teammates | 2 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |
| Help centers | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Custom domain | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove dimmi branding | No | Yes | Yes |
| Price | $0 | $29 per teammate | $59 per teammate |

## How seats are counted

A seat is a teammate who can sign in. Invitations don't count until they're accepted, and removing a teammate frees their seat straight away.

## Changing plans

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades apply at the end of the period.
