dimmi agent answers customers in the messenger and by email using only what you've told it: your help center, the sources you connect and the instructions you write. It never makes up a policy or a feature.

## Where answers come from

For each question, dimmi agent searches your published articles, your changelog and any knowledge sources you've connected, like your docs site or a GitHub repository. It writes an answer from the passages it found and cites them, so customers can read the full article.

## When it hands over

dimmi agent hands a conversation to your team when:

- It can't find an answer in your sources.
- The customer asks for a person.
- The question matches a topic you've marked as human only, like refunds or legal requests.

When it hands over, it leaves a private note summarizing the question and what it already tried, and the conversation goes to the inbox like any other.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> dimmi agent only uses articles that are published and public. Drafts and articles marked **Agent only** are used for answers but never shown or linked to customers.

## See why it answered

Every answer in the inbox has a **Sources** link that shows the passages it used and how confident it was. If an answer is wrong, fix the article it came from; the next answer uses the new version straight away.
