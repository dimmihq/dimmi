dimmi agent runs on the AI provider you choose, with your own API key. You pay your provider directly, at their prices, and dimmi never marks up a token.

## Connect a key

1. Create an API key with your provider.
2. Open **Settings → AI** and paste it.
3. Choose the model for answers. We recommend the provider's most capable model for answers and a faster one for classifying conversations.

Your key is encrypted at rest and only used for your workspace's requests.

## Set a spending cap

Set a monthly cap under **Settings → AI → Spending**. When dimmi agent reaches it, it stops answering and conversations go straight to your team until the next month starts.
