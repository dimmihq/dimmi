Support works best as a team sport. Invite everyone who answers customers, and anyone who should see what customers are saying.

## Send an invitation

1. Open **Settings → Members**.
2. Select **Invite members** and enter one or more email addresses.
3. Pick a role for them and select **Send invitations**.

Each person gets an email with a link that's valid for seven days. You can resend or revoke an invitation from the same page while it's pending.

## Roles

| Role | Can answer conversations | Can change settings | Can manage billing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Admin | Yes | Yes | No |
| Member | Yes | No | No |

> [!NOTE]
> Every workspace has at least one owner. To leave a workspace you own, make someone else an owner first.

## Sign in with your company account

If your team signs in with Google or Microsoft, turn on **Allowed domains** and anyone with an address on your domain can join without an invitation. They join as members; you can change their role afterwards.
