Unassigned conversations are everyone's problem, which means they're nobody's. Assignment makes sure each conversation has one person responsible for it.

## Assign by hand

Open a conversation and choose a teammate from the **Assignee** menu, or press <kbd>A</kbd>. The teammate gets a notification, and the conversation moves to their **Assigned to me** view.

## Round robin

Turn on **Round robin** for an inbox and new conversations go to the teammate who's online and has the fewest open conversations. Teammates who set themselves away are skipped until they're back.

## Rules

Rules assign or tag conversations as they arrive. A rule has conditions and actions:

- **When** the channel is email **and** the subject contains "invoice", **assign to** the Billing inbox.
- **When** the customer's plan is Enterprise, **add the tag** `priority`.

Rules run top to bottom, and the first one that assigns a conversation wins.
