Your inbox should only show conversations that need you right now.

## Snooze

Snooze a conversation until a time, like tomorrow morning, or until the customer replies. It leaves the inbox and comes back with a dot when the time comes or the customer writes.

## Close

Close a conversation when the customer has what they need. Closed conversations stay searchable, and they reopen on their own if the customer writes again.
