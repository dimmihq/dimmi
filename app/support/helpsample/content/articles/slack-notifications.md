title: Get notified in Slack
description: Post new conversations, mentions and changelog entries to the Slack channels your team already watches.
collection: integrations
author: maya
updated: 2026-09-28
---
Connect Slack from **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and choose what goes where. Most teams post new conversations from Enterprise customers to a `#vip-support` channel and new changelog entries to `#announcements`.

Each notification links straight to the conversation in dimmi, and you can reply from Slack with the **Reply** button.
