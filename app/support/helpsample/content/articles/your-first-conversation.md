Open any conversation from the inbox. The customer's messages are on the left, your replies on the right, and everything dimmi knows about the customer sits in the panel beside them.

## Reply or add a note

Type in the composer and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send. Switch to **Note** to write something only your team sees, and mention a teammate with `@` to bring them in.

## Assign and close

Assign the conversation to yourself so nobody else replies at the same time. Once the customer has what they need, close it. If they write back, it reopens with the whole history.

> [!TIP]
> Press <kbd>S</kbd> to snooze a conversation until the customer replies. It leaves your inbox now and comes back the moment they answer.
