The messenger should feel like part of your product, not a widget bolted onto it. Everything below lives in **Settings → Messenger**, and the preview beside the settings updates as you type.

## Brand color

Pick the color your buttons and launcher use. dimmi checks the contrast and picks black or white text on top of it, so labels stay readable whatever you choose.

## Launcher

Choose a round or square launcher, place it bottom right or bottom left, and give it a label like **Help** or **Chat with us** if you'd rather not show the icon alone.

## Greeting and tabs

The greeting is the first thing customers read when they open the messenger. Keep it short and specific:

- **Good:** "Hi there 👋 Ask us anything about billing or your account."
- **Too vague:** "How can we help you today?"

Then choose which tabs to show and in what order: **Home**, **Messages**, **Help** and **Changelog**. Turn off the ones you don't need yet; customers never see an empty tab.

## Sound

The messenger plays a soft sound when it opens and when a reply arrives while it's closed. Turn it off if your product is used in quiet places, like a library app or a meeting tool.

> [!NOTE]
> Changes reach every page with the messenger within a minute. Customers who already have it open see them the next time they load a page.
