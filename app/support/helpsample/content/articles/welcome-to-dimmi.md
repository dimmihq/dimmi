dimmi brings every conversation with your customers into one place: chats from the messenger on your site, emails to your support address, and messages from Slack or Discord communities. Your team answers them together from a shared inbox, and dimmi agent answers the questions your help center already covers.

This guide walks through the four parts of dimmi and the order we recommend setting them up in.

## The shared inbox

Every conversation lands in the inbox, whatever channel it came from. Teammates see who is replying, assign conversations to each other, leave private notes and snooze a conversation until the customer answers.

> [!TIP]
> Start with one inbox for the whole team. You can split it into inboxes per team or product later, and conversations move with their history intact.

## The messenger

The messenger is the chat bubble on your site or app. Customers start a conversation from it, read your help articles without leaving the page and see what's new in your product.

Install it with one script tag, or with the package for your framework. It weighs under 35 KB and loads after your page does, so it never slows your site down.

## The help center

Your help center is a public site of articles your customers can search, at a subdomain like `help.acme.com` or under your own domain. Articles are written in Markdown, so you can draft them in your editor, import them from GitHub or let dimmi agent suggest them from resolved conversations.

## dimmi agent

dimmi agent reads your help center and answers customers in the messenger and by email. When it can't answer with confidence, it hands the conversation to your team with a summary, so nobody has to ask the customer to repeat themselves.

## What to set up first

1. [Invite your team](/articles/invite-your-team) so you're not answering alone.
2. [Install the messenger](/articles/install-on-nextjs) on your site.
3. [Connect your support email](/articles/connect-your-support-email) so emails land in the same inbox.
4. Write three or four articles for the questions you answer most often.
5. Turn on dimmi agent once your help center covers the basics.
