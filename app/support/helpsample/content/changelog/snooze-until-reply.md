Waiting on a customer? Choose **Until they reply** when you snooze, and the conversation leaves your inbox until they write back, however long that takes.

## Fixed

- Desktop notifications no longer repeat when you have dimmi open in two tabs.
- Mentions in notes notify the mentioned teammate even when they've muted the conversation.
