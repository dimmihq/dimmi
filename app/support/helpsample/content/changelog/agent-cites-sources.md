Customers trust answers they can check. dimmi agent now ends every answer with links to the articles it used, and customers can open them right in the messenger.

## Sources in the inbox

In the inbox, each answer has a **Sources** link showing the passages dimmi agent read, ranked by how much they contributed. When an answer is wrong, you can see which article misled it and fix it in one click.

## Better at saying "I don't know"

dimmi agent now hands over when its sources don't clearly answer the question, instead of stretching a nearby article to fit. In our own workspace, handovers went up 6% and wrong answers went down by more than half.
