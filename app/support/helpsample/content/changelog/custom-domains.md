Your help center can now live on your own domain. Add `help.acme.com` in settings, add two DNS records, and dimmi takes care of the rest, including the certificate.

Your changelog moves with it to `help.acme.com/changelog`, and every old link on the dimmi subdomain redirects to the new address, so nothing you've shared breaks.
