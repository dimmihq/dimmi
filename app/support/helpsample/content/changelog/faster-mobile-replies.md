We rebuilt how the inbox loads on phones. Conversations now open in under 300 ms on a mid-range Android phone, down from 700 ms.

- The composer stays above the keyboard on iOS Safari instead of hiding behind it.
- Swiping a conversation left snoozes it, and swiping right closes it.
- Images in conversations load at the size your screen needs, not the size they were uploaded at.
