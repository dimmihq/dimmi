Some problems are easier to say than to type. Customers can now hold the microphone button in the messenger to record a voice note of up to two minutes.

Your team plays it inline in the inbox, at up to double speed, and dimmi transcribes it when you've connected an AI key, so voice notes show up in search like any other message.

Turn voice notes off in **Settings → Messenger → Attachments** if you'd rather keep to text.
