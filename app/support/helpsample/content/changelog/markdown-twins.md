More of your customers are asking an AI assistant before they ask you. Now those assistants can read your help center properly.

- Add `.md` to any article's address to get it as clean Markdown.
- Your help center publishes `/llms.txt`, an index of every article, and `/llms-full.txt` with all of them in one file.
- Pages tell crawlers where the Markdown version lives, so they don't have to scrape your HTML.
