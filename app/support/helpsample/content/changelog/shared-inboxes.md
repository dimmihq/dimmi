As teams grow, one inbox for everything stops working. Billing questions sit next to bug reports, and nobody's sure whose turn it is. Today you can split your workspace into as many inboxes as you need.

## Inboxes with their own teammates

Each inbox has its own members, so the billing team sees billing conversations and nothing else. Teammates who belong to several inboxes switch between them from the sidebar, and **All inboxes** still shows everything at once.

## Rules route conversations as they arrive

Rules now move conversations between inboxes. Send emails to `billing@` to the Billing inbox, chats from Enterprise customers to Priority, and Spanish conversations to the team that speaks Spanish.

## Office hours per inbox

Every inbox can have its own office hours and timezone. The messenger tells customers when to expect a reply based on the inbox their conversation is in.

> [!TIP]
> Existing conversations stay in your first inbox. Move a batch by selecting them and choosing **Move to inbox**.
